TrailForge v0.5.0, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine (SDL3 + SDL GPU on Metal, Jolt
Physics `WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio). This stage reworks the grass: several
grass species, flower clusters, a wheat species with a small test patch, and wind that travels across the meadow as
gusts. The physics is unchanged from v0.4.1; grass, flowers and wheat are visual only.

**Platform:** macOS 27 on Apple Silicon (arm64) only. The binary is built for macOS 27.0
(`LSMinimumSystemVersion` 27.0); nothing else has been run.

## What is new

- **Lusher grass.** Blades grow in clumps with dense patches and a few sparse spots, curve from a wider base to a
  pointed tip, are darker at the root and lighter at the tip, vary in hue and value per blade and per clump, have
  rounded normals so they read as volumes, and let the sun shine through them (warm glow at sunset when you look
  toward the sun).
- **Grass species that blend by zone:** short lawn grass, tall wild grass (part of it with seed-head panicles),
  low clover leaves, and dry straw-coloured patches on slopes and in sparse margins near rocks and dirt.
- **Flowers here and there:** clusters of white daisies, yellow buttercups, purple-blue bellflowers, clover heads
  and a few red poppies, plus rare lone flowers. They sway with their stems.
- **Wheat:** tall stalks with ears and awns, golden to green-yellow, sown densely in rows. Wind gusts roll over the
  field as bright waves, and the wheels flatten a visible lane. A small test patch sits in the west meadow
  (teleport "Wheat patch (visual)" in F1 → Teleport); the real field comes with a later level.
- **Wind as travelling gusts** across the meadow instead of every blade jittering on its own.
- **No visible grass ring at distance:** the ground under the grass takes the matching canopy colour where the
  blades thin out.
- **Wheels still flatten everything** (grass, flowers, wheat), and torn turf still removes blades.
- **Graphics presets:** Low and Medium keep the same blade counts as before; flower density (0.5 / 0.8 / 1 / 1) and
  fine detail (awns, seed-head spikelets, clover marks; off on Low) are set per preset. F2 → Graphics has "Flowers"
  and "Grass detail".

Measured on the development Mac (2880x1800, vsync off, alternating runs of v0.4.1 and v0.5.0): Low and Medium GPU
time within the run-to-run spread (about ±0.4 ms), High +0.2 to +0.6 ms, Ultra +0.4 to +1.9 ms (most in the view
over the meadow and the wheat). CPU time unchanged.

| Throttle / brake (stopped: release S and press again for reverse) | W / S | RT / LT |

## How to open it

The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.5.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

## Known limitations

- macOS 27 / Apple Silicon only; Metal only. Not notarized.
- The new grass, flowers, wheat and wind were judged from still captures only, not watched in motion by a person.
- Grass, flowers and wheat are render-only: no drag, no rolling resistance, no grip change; the ground under the
  wheat is the ordinary grass surface. Only the wheel contacts and the tire marks flatten blades (not the body).
- From a high camera Low and Medium still look like scattered blades (same blade count as before).
- Ultra costs up to about 1.9 ms more GPU time in grass-heavy views.
- All v0.4.x limitations still apply: ABS and TC are simplified logic, "locked" differentials are a strong torque
  bias, the ground is rigid (sand and mud are lower-friction surfaces only), handling and sound have not been judged
  by hand or by ear, and gamepad support has not been tested with a real controller.
