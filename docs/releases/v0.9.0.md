TrailForge v0.9.0, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine (SDL3 + SDL GPU, Jolt Physics
`WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio). This stage adds a shared vehicle style kit,
rebuilds the SUV as car #1 of the concept sheet, and fits it with bigger 315/70R17 tires.

**Platforms:**

| Package | Status |
|---|---|
| `TrailForge-0.9.0-macos-arm64.zip` | macOS 27 on Apple Silicon. **Run and verified** (built for macOS 27.0). |
| `TrailForge-0.9.0-windows-x64.zip` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (MSVC, x64); the D3D12 / Vulkan rendering has never been run. |
| `TrailForge-0.9.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (gcc, Ubuntu); the app was only started on Mesa lavapipe (software Vulkan) under Xvfb. |

## What is new

- **New SUV body.** A procedural five-door expedition SUV modeled on car #1 of the concept sheet:
  - lofted body with chamfers, a belt crease and a tapered greenhouse (raked windshield, inset glass);
  - big black flares, cladding, rock sliders, deep steel bumpers with a winch;
  - LED daytime running lights and taillights, olive paint with orange accents and a white roof;
  - roof rack with a spare wheel, jerry cans and cases, snorkel and rear carrier.

  About 16.7k triangles (7.1k before), 43 named parts. Antennas are left out on purpose.
- **Style kit** for the next cars:
  - materials: clear-coat paint without flakes, matte plastic, metal, rubber, glass, LEDs;
  - two-tone liveries and decal stripes;
  - lofted surfaces with hard chamfers, panel gaps, lights and accessories;
  - a parametric wheel library;
  - named parts with `deformable` / `detachable` flags (no damage logic yet).
- **Wheels are the physics wheels.** The tire mesh has exactly the physics radius and width. New optional visual
  keys `rimRatio` and `tread` in `vehicle.json`. The arches, flares and wheel-house walls are built around the
  tire swept over the full suspension travel and steering lock, and they rebuild when the tire size changes.
  Tested for several tire sizes (no body clipping, mesh size equals the physics wheel).
- **315/70R17 tires** (35-inch class, as on the concept): 0.873 m instead of 0.80 m, 43 kg per wheel, inertia
  4.7 kg·m². The final drive is re-geared 3.90 → 4.27, so road speed per rpm stays within 0.5 %. The body rides
  3.6 cm higher. Measured with the headless probe:
  - stop from 50 km/h: 11.4 m (was 12.2 m); the front wheels no longer lock with the unchanged brakes;
  - 0-30 km/h on concrete: 1.91 s in 2H (was 1.97 s), 1.53 s in 4H;
  - 15° mud ramp: 4H tops it in 6.1 s, 4L in 7.0 s (was 7.2 s), 2H still cannot.
- **Menus and garage:**
  - the mouse wheel scrolls lists;
  - spring and damper equivalents (N/mm, kN·s/m) are shown next to the Hz and damping-ratio values;
  - rename / delete / reset of setups use a modal dialog.

## How to open it

**macOS.** The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.9.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

**Windows (untested on real hardware).** Unzip, keep the `assets` folder next to `trailforge.exe`, run it. The exe
is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn ("More info" → "Run anyway"). Needs a Direct3D 12 or Vulkan GPU driver.

**Linux (untested on real hardware).** `tar xzf TrailForge-0.9.0-linux-x64.tar.gz`, then
`./TrailForge-0.9.0-linux-x64/trailforge`. Needs a Vulkan driver (Mesa RADV / ANV / NVK or the NVIDIA driver).

## Known limitations

- Windows and Linux have never been run on real hardware; the Direct3D 12 path has not been run at all.
- Menus and garage were not tested with a real gamepad. Handling and looks were judged only from captures and
  headless measurements, not by hand.
- The body rides higher and the arch gap at rest is larger than on the concept sheet. Both follow the physics
  suspension travel, which was not changed.
- The tire and wheel masses are estimates from typical listed weights, and the inertia is a hand calculation.
  The brakes were not upgraded for the bigger tires.
- Only one of the four concept cars is built. Part flags are markers for a future damage stage; there is no
  damage yet.
- Vehicle draw cost: no difference measurable against v0.8.0 within the run-to-run spread (up to 0.5 ms GPU at
  2880x1800).
- macOS build not notarized; Windows build not code-signed.
- Earlier limitations still apply:
  - grass, spray and body mud are render-only;
  - ABS and TC are simplified logic;
  - the ground is rigid (sand and mud are lower-friction surfaces only);
  - the 4H / 4L center lock is a strong limited-slip setting.
