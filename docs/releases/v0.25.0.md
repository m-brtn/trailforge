**Superseded by v0.25.1**, which renames the circuit to SUNCOAST RACEWAY (fictional name; the layout is traced from
OpenStreetMap data of a real circuit). Use v0.25.1.

TrailForge v0.25.0, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine (SDL3 + SDL GPU, Jolt Physics
`WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio). This stage adds a race circuit (named
Suncoast Raceway since v0.25.1), laid out after OpenStreetMap data of a real circuit, and a Blender pipeline for editing levels.

**Platforms:**

| Package | Status |
|---|---|
| `TrailForge-0.25.0-macos-arm64.zip` | macOS 27 on Apple Silicon. **Run and verified** (built for macOS 27.0). |

There are no Windows or Linux packages for this release: CI is paused (GitHub Actions billing), so nothing was
built or tested on those platforms. Windows and Linux have never been run on real hardware in any release.

## What is new

- **A race circuit** (in the LEVEL row; renamed **SUNCOAST RACEWAY** in v0.25.1). An asphalt circuit; its
  layout is traced from OpenStreetMap data of a real circuit (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL). The
  ground heights come from the Copernicus GLO-30 elevation model. The full course is 1979 m along the OSM line and 1989 m as built in the game. The
  published length is 2.21 km; the 10 % gap is not explained. The circuit runs clockwise. It has an asphalt track
  10–14 m wide with edge lines, a start grid, red / white curbs, gravel and asphalt run-offs, grass, armco and tyre
  walls, a pit lane with garages, two grandstands, marshal posts, a race-control building and a start gantry with
  lights. Trees grow only outside the circuit. There is no mud, water or jumps.
- **Lap timer.** It has 3 sectors and checkpoints. The HUD shows the current lap, the sector splits and the best lap.
  Best laps are stored per level and vehicle. **R** puts the vehicle back at the last checkpoint passed, and the
  lap keeps running. Teleports, rebuilds and handing over between the autopilot and the player stop the lap. The
  mini handling track on the proving ground now uses the same timer and HUD.
- **Map and minimap** show the circuit and its POIs: start / finish, pit lane, hairpin, drag strip, sectors 2 and 3.
- **Blender level pipeline** (Blender 5.3, headless). A level can be generated as a `.blend`, edited there
  (terrain, surface masks, road / circuit curves, placements, spawn, gates) and exported back to the baked files:
  a 16-bit heightmap, surface classes and a layout. The ground you drive on and the ground you see come from the
  same baked data. A test imports and re-exports the circuit and gets identical files. See README "Editing a level
  in Blender".

## Verification

- 93 automated tests pass in Debug and Release. New tests: the baked level loads and its layout is valid, the
  track has no bumps above 6 mm except the curbs (measured maximum 4.1 mm over 107,778 points), the SUV and EV
  autopilot complete a lap with 3 sectors, and the Blender round trip gives identical files.
- Scenario checks with 0 failures: full regression 247, circuit 36 (lap, sectors, reset to checkpoint, map, views,
  frame times), mini track 14.
- Autopilot laps (a gentle driver at 130 km/h cruise, not the vehicles' limit): SUV 2:16.32, EV 2:14.39. All four
  wheels stay on asphalt for the whole lap, with no resets.
- Frame times on the circuit (2880 × 1800 output, this Mac): Low 120 FPS, Medium 118, High 113, Ultra 94.

## How to open it

**macOS.** The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.25.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

## Known limitations

- Nobody has driven Suncoast Raceway by hand; only the autopilot laps above. The curbs and gravel traps were judged
  on still captures.
- The elevation is a 30 m surface model, smoothed into a gentle surface: real bumps, camber and curb heights are
  not known.
- The SUV and EV are the only vehicles; there is no race car.
- No Windows or Linux packages for this release; those platforms have never been run on real hardware.
- macOS build not notarized.
- Earlier limitations still apply:
  - water has no physics; deep water and the map edge put the vehicle back on a road;
  - snow, sand and mud are rigid surfaces with lower grip;
  - grass, ground cover, spray and body mud are render-only;
  - ABS and TC are simplified logic;
  - sounds were judged from capture analysis, not by ear;
  - nobody has driven with a real gamepad;
  - buildings have no interiors; Blender bodies are a test path only.
