Fix-only re-release of TrailForge v0.4.0, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine
(SDL3 + SDL GPU on Metal, Jolt Physics `WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio).
It fixes the look of the wheel spray and of the soil on the tires, and lets spray mud stick to the body. The physics
and the driver aids are unchanged from v0.4.0; everything in this release is visual only.

**Platform:** macOS 27 on Apple Silicon (arm64) only. The binary is built for macOS 27.0
(`LSMinimumSystemVersion` 27.0); nothing else has been run.

## Fixes since v0.4.0

- **Wheel spray is fine, not "potatoes".** Mud spray used to be large, uniform, opaque ellipsoids that piled up on the
  grass. Now fine droplets, specks and grains dominate by count (mud radius 1.5 mm–1.3 cm, most at the small end;
  small clods 1–2.6 cm are rare, about 1 per 100 bits; sand 0.8–4 mm without clods). Flying bits are drawn as short
  motion streaks along their velocity, with irregular soft edges, and wet mud has a slight sheen. A landing bit
  becomes a flat splat on the ground that fades within 0.06–0.5 s, so nothing piles up. In a headless mud wheelspin
  test all airborne bits are under 1.5 cm radius.
- **Spray mud sticks to the body.** The material each wheel throws (from its slip, as for the spray) coats the
  rear part of its arch, the sill and lower rear door behind a front wheel, the rear quarter and the rear bumper
  behind a rear wheel; reversing coats the front parts. Wet mud builds up within a few seconds of wheelspin (headless
  test: rear arch 0.65 after 1 s, 0.96 after 3 s); dirt and grass leave a lighter coat, dry sand only a thin dust
  film. It dries lighter over minutes, falls off very slowly, and driving through a puddle washes it. It has no mass,
  no drag and no effect on the physics.
- **Tires no longer turn white in sand or green on grass.** Each soil now has an adhesion. Dry sand loads the tread
  to 0.15 of a mud load, stays in the grooves and at the sidewall base as a muted tan dust (the rubber stays mostly
  black) and falls off within about a meter on hard ground. Grass leaves the brown earth from under the turf with a
  few sparse green specks of torn blades. Wet mud remains the heaviest, darkest and longest-lasting; packed dirt is
  in between. The soil prints on hard ground follow the same amounts (after sand a faint print for ~1 m).

## Controls

Unchanged from v0.4.0:

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Throttle / brake (stopped: release S and press again for reverse) | W / S | RT / LT |
| Steering | A / D | left stick |
| Handbrake | Space | A |
| Forward ↔ reverse by hand (when stopped) | Q | X |
| Low range (when stopped) | L | Y |
| ABS on / off | B | D-pad up |
| Traction control: off → on → off-road | T | D-pad right |
| Diff locks: open → rear → rear + center → all | X | D-pad down |
| Reset to the start point | R | Back |
| Follow / free camera | C | RB |
| Headlights | H | — |
| Diagnostics / graphics / audio panels | F1 / F2 / F3 | — |
| Pause / single physics step | Esc / N | Start |
| Orbit / zoom camera | right mouse + drag / wheel | — |

## How to open it

The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.4.1-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

## Known limitations

- macOS 27 / Apple Silicon only; Metal only. Not notarized.
- The new spray and body mud were judged from still captures only, not watched in motion by a person.
- Body mud is placed in fixed regions around the wheel arches, not where particles hit; spray particles still do
  not collide with the vehicle, rocks or each other.
- The green grass specks on the tires are hard to see when the wheel is in shadow.
- GPU cost of the spray at the particle cap was measured in a noisy session; it was within the run-to-run spread of
  the v0.3.0 build (e.g. High 7.95 ms before, 6.64–6.81 ms after, Ultra 12.16 ms before, 12.51–12.66 ms after).
- All v0.4.0 limitations still apply: ABS and TC are simplified logic, "locked" differentials are a strong torque
  bias, the ground is rigid (sand and mud are lower-friction surfaces only), handling and sound have not been judged
  by hand or by ear, and gamepad support has not been tested with a real controller.
