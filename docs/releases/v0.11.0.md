TrailForge v0.11.0, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine (SDL3 + SDL GPU, Jolt Physics
`WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio). This stage adds a second vehicle, the
dual-motor electric TrailForge EV, and a vehicle selection page.

**Platforms:**

| Package | Status |
|---|---|
| `TrailForge-0.11.0-macos-arm64.zip` | macOS 27 on Apple Silicon. **Run and verified** (built for macOS 27.0). |
| `TrailForge-0.11.0-windows-x64.zip` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (MSVC, x64); the D3D12 / Vulkan rendering has never been run. |
| `TrailForge-0.11.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (gcc, Ubuntu); the app was only started on Mesa lavapipe (software Vulkan) under Xvfb. |

## What is new

- **TrailForge EV**, the electric off-road SUV from the concept sheet (white / grey body, black cladding, blue
  accents and LEDs, closed front panel with a full-width light bar, low roof rails, spare on the tailgate).
  - One motor per axle with a fixed 12:1 reduction and no gearbox: front 400 N·m / 180 kW, rear 450 N·m / 220 kW,
    15000 rpm. Constant torque up to the base speed, then constant power.
  - Electronic torque split: NORMAL 40 / 60 front / rear, OFF-ROAD 50 / 50 (V). There is no low range.
  - 120 kWh battery with state of charge. Regenerative braking acts first on lift-off and under the brake pedal
    (fades out at low speed, backs off when a wheel slips or ABS acts); the friction brakes take the rest.
  - 2650 kg (650 kg more than the SUV), with a lower center of mass from the floor battery. Springs, dampers, bump
    stops, anti-roll bars and brakes are retuned for the mass.
  - The motor torque goes through the wheels and tires in Jolt (a controller subclass), not as forces on the body.
  - The numbers are estimates for the class of large electric off-road SUVs, not a copy of one vehicle.
- **Measured, EV vs gasoline SUV** (headless probe, 120 Hz, TC and ABS off unless stated):

  | | SUV | EV |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0-100 km/h | 9.68 s | 4.30 s |
  | 100-0 km/h, ABS on | 46.42 m | 46.37 m (9 % of the energy recovered) |
  | lift-off at 80 km/h | 0.63 m/s² | 1.36 m/s² (regen) |
  | cornering at 40 km/h, steer 0.45 | 7.91 m/s², roll 4.95° | 8.33 m/s², roll 2.58° |
  | 20° dirt climb, full pedal | 4.33 s (4H) | 3.88 s |
  | 15° mud climb, full pedal | 6.12 s (4H) | 6.10 s |

- **HUD for the EV:** drive mode, a state-of-charge bar, motor power in kW (negative and blue for regen) and the
  front / rear motor torque. The Telemetry window shows per-motor values and the energy used and recovered.
- **EV sound**, synthesized from the motor speed and torque: motor and gear whine, an inverter tone at low speed and
  tire road hum. In a capture the motor tone follows the simulated motor speed (median error -0.1 %). Nobody has
  judged it by ear.
- **Vehicle selection page.** Start opens SELECT VEHICLE: cards, a description, specs and the live vehicle as the
  preview. Use Left / Right, the mouse wheel or a click to choose, and Enter or a click on the selected card to
  drive. The choice is saved in `settings.cfg`, and `--vehicle ID` selects a vehicle from the command line. The
  garage keeps setups per vehicle and shows only the rows that apply, with a new MOTORS AND REGEN group for the EV.
- **SUV model fixes:** the rear bumper now covers the whole tail (only the corners showed before), and the hood
  accent stripe is on both fenders.

## How to open it

**macOS.** The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.11.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

**Windows (untested on real hardware).** Unzip, keep the `assets` folder next to `trailforge.exe`, run it. The exe
is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn ("More info" → "Run anyway"). Needs a Direct3D 12 or Vulkan GPU driver.

**Linux (untested on real hardware).** `tar xzf TrailForge-0.11.0-linux-x64.tar.gz`, then
`./TrailForge-0.11.0-linux-x64/trailforge`. Needs a Vulkan driver (Mesa RADV / ANV / NVK or the NVIDIA driver).

## Known limitations

- Windows and Linux have never been run on real hardware; the Direct3D 12 path has not been run at all.
- With traction control off, a full-pedal EV launch spins the front wheels for about 2.5 s. Press T to enable TC.
- The electric model is simplified: constant motor and regen efficiencies, no thermal limits or derating, and the
  battery is an energy counter (no voltage or internal resistance). The two wheels of an axle do not share a motor
  shaft speed; the open differential is modelled as a torque split.
- No gamepad was connected during development; the vehicle page and all gamepad bindings are untested with a pad.
- Sounds were judged from capture analysis, not by ear.
- macOS build not notarized; Windows build not code-signed.
- Earlier limitations still apply:
  - grass, spray and body mud are render-only;
  - ABS and TC are simplified logic;
  - the ground is rigid (sand and mud are lower-friction surfaces only);
  - the SUV's 4H / 4L center lock is a strong limited-slip setting.
