Fourth packaged build of TrailForge, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine
(SDL3 + SDL GPU on Metal, Jolt Physics `WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio).
This release is about driver aids: differential locks, ABS, traction control, a friction ellipse for the tires and
an auto reverse that holds the vehicle on a slope.

**Platform:** macOS 27 on Apple Silicon (arm64) only. The binary is built for macOS 27.0
(`LSMinimumSystemVersion` 27.0); nothing else has been run.

## Changes since v0.3.0

- **Differential locks.** Front, rear and center differentials each have Open / LSD / Locked. X cycles the locks:
  none → rear → rear + center → all. "Locked" is Jolt's limited-slip differential with a ratio of 1.05 (almost all
  torque goes to the slower side), not a rigid link between the wheels. On a 15° slope with the front-left wheel
  over a pit: all open stays put (the hanging wheel spins at 14 m/s), the default rear LSD gets out in 3.5 s,
  rear + center locks in 4.5 s, all locked in 3.1 s.
- **ABS** (B, off by default). Per-wheel brake pressure modulation, cycling at 9–15 Hz, with the pressure going down
  to zero. Straight stops on the flat surface lanes, ABS off → on (120 Hz):

  | Surface (peak μ) | 50 → 0 km/h | 100 → 0 km/h |
  |---|---|---|
  | Concrete (0.95) | 14.2 → 13.8 m | 52.5 → 46.6 m |
  | Rock (0.75) | 18.2 → 16.2 m | 67.3 → 58.5 m |
  | Packed dirt (0.70) | 19.4 → 17.3 m | 72.0 → 61.9 m |
  | Sand (0.55) | 24.4 → 21.5 m | 91.1 → 78.2 m |
  | Grass (0.50) | 27.1 → 23.7 m | 99.9 → 86.1 m |
  | Mud (0.35) | 39.6 → 34.4 m | 141.8 → 118.0 m |

  With ABS the wheels are locked for at most 0.16 wheel-seconds per stop, and the car steers while braking (40 %
  steer, heading after 1 s at 50 km/h: concrete −19° → −27°, mud −6° → −14°).
- **Traction control** (T, off by default): Off → On → Off-road. It brakes a wheel that spins ahead of the others
  (which sends an open differential's torque to the wheel with grip) and cuts the throttle when all wheels spin.
  15° mud hill start (μ 0.35) at full throttle, 9 runs per mode (60 / 120 / 240 Hz × 3 start points): TC off
  reaches the top 5 of 9 times, On 6 of 9 (10.6–11.9 s at 120 Hz), Off-road 6 of 9. At 60 Hz TC is worse than off.
- **Friction ellipse** (on by default, F1 → Tuning). Jolt limits the longitudinal and lateral tire forces
  independently, so a locked wheel used to steer as well as a rolling one. Past the grip peak, the two limits are
  now scaled by their share of the combined slip. Off → on: straight stops and launches unchanged (within 0.2 m),
  cornering at 30 % steer unchanged (concrete 0.85 g), full lock 0.72 → 0.65 g on concrete, and a locked car steered
  on concrete turns −32° → −19° in 1 s. Without the ellipse, a car with locked wheels left its lane on dirt, sand,
  grass and mud.
- **Auto reverse holds.** An S press that started while moving keeps braking through the stop and holds the vehicle,
  also on a slope (0.8 mm of creep in 6 s on the 12° hill). Reverse engages only after S is released and pressed
  again at a standstill; W works the same way in reverse.
- **HUD and UI.** ABS / TC lamps (dim = off, green = armed, blinking amber = acting), the mode of each differential
  (F / C / R), hints for every switch, telemetry rows for brake torque, ABS pressure and TC brake, and all aid
  parameters in F1 → Tuning → Driver aids.
- **Test track.** A sand lane in the surface lanes layout; the lanes are now 1.1 km long.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Throttle / brake (stopped: release S and press again for reverse) | W / S | RT / LT |
| Steering | A / D | left stick |
| Handbrake | Space | A |
| Forward ↔ reverse by hand (when stopped) | Q | X |
| Low range (when stopped) | L | Y |
| ABS on / off | B | D-pad up |
| Traction control: off → on → off-road | T | D-pad right |
| Diff locks: open → rear → rear + center → all | X | D-pad down |
| Reset to the start point | R | Back |
| Follow / free camera | C | RB |
| Headlights | H | — |
| Diagnostics / graphics / audio panels | F1 / F2 / F3 | — |
| Pause / single physics step | Esc / N | Start |
| Orbit / zoom camera | right mouse + drag / wheel | — |

ABS and TC switches survive R and teleports; the diff locks reset to none.

## How to open it

The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.4.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

## Known limitations

- macOS 27 / Apple Silicon only; Metal only. Not notarized.
- ABS and TC are simplified control logic, not models of production units. They read the true ground speed of each
  contact point (a real system has to estimate it) and act only through brake torque and a throttle factor. At
  60 Hz both are coarser; TC at 60 Hz slides back on the 15° mud hill. TC On fails the 20° grass hill start that
  TC off and Off-road climb; 20° mud fails in every mode.
- "Locked" differentials are a strong torque bias, not a rigid link: rear-only locks do not get out of the pit slope.
- The friction ellipse is a simple normalized-slip ellipse (no load sensitivity, no relaxation length). It fades in
  between 1 and 3 m/s, so it does nothing at a crawl.
- Hill starts near the grip limit are chaotic: a tiny change can flip a single run, so the results above are sweeps.
- The ground is still rigid: sand and mud are lower-friction surfaces only. Tire marks, tread soil, vehicle dirt and
  wheel spray are visual only.
- Handling, the aids and sound have not been judged by hand or by ear. All checks are automated (scenario scripts
  and headless measurements). Gamepad support, including the D-pad bindings, has not been tested with a real
  controller.
