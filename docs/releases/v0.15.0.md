TrailForge v0.15.0, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine (SDL3 + SDL GPU, Jolt Physics
`WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio). This stage rebuilds **both cars from the
concept sheet**: the bodies are traced from the four drawn views of `Cars.png`, so their proportions and form follow
the drawing, and the body collision now comes from the same traced shape.

**Platforms:**

| Package | Status |
|---|---|
| `TrailForge-0.15.0-macos-arm64.zip` | macOS 27 on Apple Silicon. **Run and verified** (built for macOS 27.0). |
| `TrailForge-0.15.0-windows-x64.zip` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (MSVC, x64); the D3D12 / Vulkan rendering has never been run. |
| `TrailForge-0.15.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (gcc, Ubuntu); the app was only started on Mesa lavapipe (software Vulkan) under Xvfb, without loading the level. |

## What is new

- **Cars traced from the concept sheet.** A tool reads the side, top, front and rear drawings of each car, scales
  them to the real tire size and writes a blueprint. The game builds the body from the blueprint around the physics
  wheels. Silhouette match with the drawing (1.0 = identical), side / front / rear / top:

  | Car | v0.14.0 | v0.15.0 |
  |---|---|---|
  | Expedition SUV | 0.72 / 0.72 / 0.71 / 0.83 | 0.90 / 0.91 / 0.91 / 0.89 |
  | Electric SUV | 0.69 / 0.75 / 0.71 / 0.84 | 0.95 / 0.90 / 0.89 / 0.97 |

- **Concept proportions.** Wheelbase and track now match the drawing: SUV 2.56 m / 1.75 m, EV 2.44 m / 1.77 m (both
  were 2.8 m / 1.80 m). Mass, engine, motors and suspension are unchanged.
- **Wheel arches hug the tires.** Each wheelhouse is the space the tire needs over its full suspension travel and
  steering lock, with a thin fender lip around it. The suspension stays visible: the orange coil-overs of the SUV and
  the blue ones of the EV, as on the sheet, move with the physics suspension every frame.
- **Accessories as drawn.** Raised fender tops, winch (SUV), tow hooks, mirrors, roof rails with a light bar and roof
  cases, rock sliders, skid plates, rear jerry cans and tailgate spare (SUV). No antennas, no roof spare, no mud
  flaps, no snorkel or ladder (not on the sheet), no spare on the EV (none drawn).
- **Collision from the same shape.** The body collision is built from the traced body instead of three boxes. On the
  diagonal twister the body no longer touches the ground (SUV 34 → 0, EV 30 → 0 of 1200 physics steps).
- **Hood camera** moved to the new, lower roof lines. The hood fills the bottom 19% (SUV) and 20% (EV) of the view.
- **Driving numbers** (headless measurements, v0.14.0 → v0.15.0):

  | | SUV | EV |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0–100 km/h | 9.68 → 9.58 s | 4.36 → 4.42 s |
  | 100–0 km/h with ABS | 46.4 → 47.3 m | 46.4 → 46.4 m |
  | lateral grip at 40 km/h (steer 0.25 / 0.45) | 6.9 / 7.9 → 7.4 / 7.8 m/s² | 7.3 / 8.3 → 8.2 / 8.2 m/s² |
  | 20° dirt climb, full pedal | 4.33 → 4.34 s | 3.88 → 3.82 s |

## How to open it

**macOS.** The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.15.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

**Windows (untested on real hardware).** Unzip, keep the `assets` folder next to `trailforge.exe`, run it. The exe
is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn ("More info" → "Run anyway"). Needs a Direct3D 12 or Vulkan GPU driver.

**Linux (untested on real hardware).** `tar xzf TrailForge-0.15.0-linux-x64.tar.gz`, then
`./TrailForge-0.15.0-linux-x64/trailforge`. Needs a Vulkan driver (Mesa RADV / ANV / NVK or the NVIDIA driver).

## Known limitations

- **Paint is blotchy up close.** Colours come from the drawings per small triangle, so colour edges are stepped, and
  where one drawing paints an accessory over the body (the bull bar over the hood in the top view, cladding and
  handles in the side view) its dark colour lands on the panel.
- The SUV side view (0.8998) and the EV rear view (0.89) are just under the 0.9 silhouette target. The sheet itself
  is not consistent: the end views are drawn at their own scale, the end-view tires are drawn much wider than the
  side-view ones, and the drawn flares are narrower than the tires.
- The body collision is a set of convex pieces: concave details are filled in, and accessories (roof load, winch,
  spare) have no collision.
- Nobody has judged the new cars by eye beyond still captures.
- Windows and Linux have never been run on real hardware; the Direct3D 12 path has not been run at all.
- macOS build not notarized; Windows build not code-signed.
- Earlier limitations still apply:
  - water has no physics; deep water and the map edge put the vehicle back on a road;
  - snow, sand and mud are rigid surfaces with lower grip;
  - grass, spray and body mud are render-only;
  - ABS and TC are simplified logic;
  - sounds were judged from capture analysis, not by ear;
  - nobody has driven with a real gamepad.
