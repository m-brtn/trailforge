TrailForge v0.19.0, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine (SDL3 + SDL GPU, Jolt Physics
`WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio). This stage is the second part of the **world
look** work on Exploration Peaks: post effects, a scattering sky with moving clouds, and new water shading. The
physics did not change.

**Platforms:**

| Package | Status |
|---|---|
| `TrailForge-0.19.0-macos-arm64.zip` | macOS 27 on Apple Silicon. **Run and verified** (built for macOS 27.0). |
| `TrailForge-0.19.0-windows-x64.zip` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (MSVC, x64); the D3D12 / Vulkan rendering has never been run. |
| `TrailForge-0.19.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (gcc, Ubuntu); the app was only started on Mesa lavapipe (software Vulkan) under Xvfb, without loading the level. |

## What is new

- **Post effects.** Each one has a control in F2 → Graphics → "Post effects" and a default per quality preset:
  - temporal anti-aliasing with sharpening; High and Ultra now use it instead of MSAA, and Low keeps FXAA;
  - ground-truth ambient occlusion at half resolution;
  - motion blur from the camera and from the moving vehicle parts (off on Low);
  - bloom with lens flare ghosts, and sun shafts;
  - eye adaptation;
  - AgX tone mapping (ACES selectable) with a colour grade toward the key-art mood;
  - vignette and subtle film grain; chromatic aberration (off by default);
  - depth of field on the vehicle in the menus and the garage, optionally also while driving.
- **Sky.** The sky colour comes from a simple scattering model of the air, so it is blue overhead, pale at the
  horizon and warm toward a low sun. It is blended with the old palette, which still drives night and twilight.
  Distant land fades into the sky colour behind it. On open levels a thin valley fog sits over the sea and the low
  ground, thicker in the morning. Clouds drift across the sky, and their shadows move over the ground and the water.
- **Water.** Lakes, the river and the sea have:
  - wind waves that are calmer in the shallows;
  - see-through shallows: the bed shows through and is tinted by depth;
  - reflections of the scene (screen-space, falling back to the sky);
  - foam along the shore.

  Seen from above, a waterfall's upper sheet now looks like glassy water instead of a white panel. The waterfall mist
  fades softly where it meets rocks and banks. All of this is render-only; there are still no water physics.
- **Performance** at 2880 × 1800, measured on five views (village, forest, lake, canyon, peaks):

  | Preset | Range | Lowest view |
  |---|---|---|
  | Low | 120 fps at every view (the display cap) | — |
  | Medium | 93–120 fps | forest |
  | High | 61–120 fps | forest, 61.6 fps |
  | Ultra | 42–120 fps | forest |

  The new effects together cost about 5 ms per frame on High while driving (measured per pass, see the devlog).

## How to open it

**macOS.** The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.19.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

**Windows (untested on real hardware).** Unzip, keep the `assets` folder next to `trailforge.exe`, run it. The exe
is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn ("More info" → "Run anyway"). Needs a Direct3D 12 or Vulkan GPU driver.

**Linux (untested on real hardware).** `tar xzf TrailForge-0.19.0-linux-x64.tar.gz`, then
`./TrailForge-0.19.0-linux-x64/trailforge`. Needs a Vulkan driver (Mesa RADV / ANV / NVK or the NVIDIA driver).

## Known limitations

- Everything new was judged on still captures only, not in motion. That includes TAA ghosting, motion blur, the
  drifting clouds and the waves.
- High drops to about 62 fps in the dense forest, and Ultra to 42 fps there.
- Depth of field costs about 4 ms. It is only on in the menus and the garage unless "DOF while driving" is raised.
- Screen-space reflections cannot show what is off screen; there the water reflects the sky.
- The waves do not follow the river current.
- The sky model ignores light that scatters more than once.
- Not done yet:
  - a better horizon;
  - boulders at the foot of the canyon walls;
  - more rock layering on the canyon walls;
  - a darker forest floor.
- Windows and Linux have never been run on real hardware; the Direct3D 12 path has not been run at all. The new
  shaders were compiled for Direct3D only by CI.
- macOS build not notarized; Windows build not code-signed.
- Earlier limitations still apply:
  - water has no physics; deep water and the map edge put the vehicle back on a road;
  - snow, sand and mud are rigid surfaces with lower grip;
  - grass, ground cover, spray and body mud are render-only;
  - ABS and TC are simplified logic;
  - sounds were judged from capture analysis, not by ear;
  - nobody has driven with a real gamepad.
