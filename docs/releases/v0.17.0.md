TrailForge v0.17.0, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine (SDL3 + SDL GPU, Jolt Physics
`WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio). This stage **repaints both cars**: clean
panels with straight colour borders, as on the concept sheet, instead of the blotchy camouflage look of v0.15 / v0.16.

**Platforms:**

| Package | Status |
|---|---|
| `TrailForge-0.17.0-macos-arm64.zip` | macOS 27 on Apple Silicon. **Run and verified** (built for macOS 27.0). |
| `TrailForge-0.17.0-windows-x64.zip` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (MSVC, x64); the D3D12 / Vulkan rendering has never been run. |
| `TrailForge-0.17.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (gcc, Ubuntu); the app was only started on Mesa lavapipe (software Vulkan) under Xvfb, without loading the level. |

## What is new

- **Paint regions.** The paint of the gasoline SUV and the electric SUV now comes from clean regions per concept
  view. The SUV is olive with black lower cladding, bumpers and arches, a white roof and orange accents only where the
  sheet draws them. The EV is white with black cladding and blue accents. The tracer cleans the sheet's colours:
  accessories drawn over the body (bull bar, rack, handles, jerry cans) are filled with the body colour around
  them, and small specks are removed. Hand-drawn polygons give the main panels, the window band and the cladding line
  straight edges.
- **Straight borders.** Every point of the body takes its colour from the one concept view that actually sees it.
  Triangles are cut where the colour changes, so the borders run straight through the mesh instead of in 2 cm steps.
  The dark blotches on the hood and doors and the olive bands in the side windows are gone. In the hood camera, the
  SUV's dark strokes and the jagged edge of the EV's hood insert are gone too.
- **Materials.** Body paint keeps its clear coat. The cladding, bumpers and fender flares are a grained matte
  plastic. The glass is dark and reflective.
- **Physics unchanged.** Body shape, collision, wheel openings and the visible suspension are the same as in v0.16.0.
  The headless measurements (braking, launch, cornering, climbing, the 0–100 / 100–0 comparison, the bench run) give
  identical results for both cars.
- The body meshes have about 26–30% more triangles from the cut borders (SUV about 197k, EV about 185k panel
  triangles).

## How to open it

**macOS.** The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.17.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

**Windows (untested on real hardware).** Unzip, keep the `assets` folder next to `trailforge.exe`, run it. The exe
is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn ("More info" → "Run anyway"). Needs a Direct3D 12 or Vulkan GPU driver.

**Linux (untested on real hardware).** `tar xzf TrailForge-0.17.0-linux-x64.tar.gz`, then
`./TrailForge-0.17.0-linux-x64/trailforge`. Needs a Vulkan driver (Mesa RADV / ANV / NVK or the NVIDIA driver).

## Known limitations

- The fender flares are plain black plastic. Where the concept's views disagree at a corner (the SUV's hood front
  corners, the EV's rear quarter above the wheel), a small patch takes the colour of one view.
- The paint polygons were drawn by hand on the concept sheet; they are accurate to about 1 cm.
- Only the door shut lines are drawn as panel lines; there are no window frame or seam lines yet.
- Nobody has judged the new paint by eye beyond the captures; it was checked on still images only.
- Windows and Linux have never been run on real hardware; the Direct3D 12 path has not been run at all.
- macOS build not notarized; Windows build not code-signed.
- Earlier limitations still apply:
  - water has no physics; deep water and the map edge put the vehicle back on a road;
  - snow, sand and mud are rigid surfaces with lower grip;
  - grass, spray and body mud are render-only;
  - ABS and TC are simplified logic;
  - the gamepad map controls were tested with a virtual pad only; the attract drive is silent;
  - sounds were judged from capture analysis, not by ear;
  - nobody has driven with a real gamepad.
