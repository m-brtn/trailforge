TrailForge v0.23.0, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine (SDL3 + SDL GPU, Jolt Physics
`WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio). This stage brings back the clean v0.14-style
car bodies, makes the glass dark and nearly opaque, removes the lamp lens-flare ghosts at night and adds a mini
handling track with a lap timer to the proving ground.

**Platforms:**

| Package | Status |
|---|---|
| `TrailForge-0.23.0-macos-arm64.zip` | macOS 27 on Apple Silicon. **Run and verified** (built for macOS 27.0). |

| `TrailForge-0.23.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (gcc, Ubuntu); the app was only started on Mesa lavapipe (software Vulkan) under Xvfb. |

## What is new

- **Clean car bodies again.** Since v0.15.0 the SUV and the EV were meshed from traced blueprints, which left
  sawtooth steps along the hood and fender edges, wavy window panels and rough wheel-arch lips. Both cars are built
  from the v0.14.0 panel kit again, with the collision boxes, 2.80 m wheelbase, 1.80 m track and hood camera of
  v0.14.0. Kept from later versions: no antennas, no mud flaps, no snorkel, ladder or roof spare, slim fender
  flares, low roof rails, the olive SUV with orange accents, visible suspension coils (orange on the SUV, blue on
  the EV), and wheels and arches that follow the physics wheel size, suspension travel and steering. The traced
  body is still available to vehicle definitions as `"bodyShape": "traced"`.
  Measured with the probe: SUV 0–100 km/h 9.68 s, 100–0 46.42 m with ABS; EV 0–100 4.36 s, 100–0 46.37 m with ABS
  (the same as v0.14.0).
- **Dark tinted glass.** Vehicle windows (procedural and Blender bodies) are dark and nearly opaque. They keep the
  sun highlight and part of the sky reflection; there is no interior behind them.
- **No lamp ghosts at night.** The lens flare used to mirror every bright light, so headlights and brake lights
  produced coloured discs and red streaks at night. Now only the sun feeds the flare (none at night, below the
  horizon or with the sun off screen).
- **Mini handling track.** A 1044 m asphalt circuit south of the proving ground: hairpin, chicane, constant-radius
  corner, slalom, sweeper, curbs, gravel traps and tyre walls. Render and collision come from the same triangles.
  Teleport "Mini handling track"; a lap timer with sector splits runs in the HUD on the proving ground. Autopilot
  probe laps: SUV 1:04.57, EV 1:01.25 (the autopilot's laps, not the cars' limits).
- **Blender test SUV** (test data only, not in the game) is olive like the shipped SUV instead of red-orange.

## How to open it

**macOS.** The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.23.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

**Windows (untested on real hardware).** Unzip, keep the `assets` folder next to `trailforge.exe`, run it. The exe
is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn ("More info" → "Run anyway"). Needs a Direct3D 12 or Vulkan GPU driver.

**Linux (untested on real hardware).** `tar xzf TrailForge-0.23.0-linux-x64.tar.gz`, then
`./TrailForge-0.23.0-linux-x64/trailforge`. Needs a Vulkan driver (Mesa RADV / ANV / NVK or the NVIDIA driver).

## Known limitations

- The restored bodies, the glass and the flare change were judged on still captures only; nobody drove the cars
  by eye after the change.
- The traced body (`bodyShape: "traced"`) is only covered by automated tests; no scenario drives it.
- Glass has no interior; it is not see-through.
- Mini track: nobody has driven it by hand or with a gamepad; gravel is the rigid sand surface; curbs are flat
  concrete strips.
- Windows and Linux have never been run on real hardware; the Direct3D 12 path has not been run at all.
- macOS build not notarized; Windows build not code-signed.
- Earlier limitations still apply:
  - water has no physics; deep water and the map edge put the vehicle back on a road;
  - snow, sand and mud are rigid surfaces with lower grip;
  - grass, ground cover, spray and body mud are render-only;
  - ABS and TC are simplified logic;
  - sounds were judged from capture analysis, not by ear;
  - nobody has driven with a real gamepad;
  - buildings have no interiors; Blender bodies are a test path only.
