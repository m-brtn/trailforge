TrailForge v0.12.0, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine (SDL3 + SDL GPU, Jolt Physics
`WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio). This stage adds levels and the first
open-world level, **Exploration Peaks**: about 2 × 2 km of streamed terrain, a 0–300 m elevation range, 13 points
of interest, rivers, a lake and the sea.

## What is new

- **Level choice.** The start menu has a LEVEL row: EXPLORATION PEAKS or PROVING GROUND. The choice is saved in
  `settings.cfg`; `--level ID` selects a level from the command line. The proving ground is unchanged and stays
  as the development level.
- **Exploration Peaks**, after the concept map:
  - snowy peaks and a pass in the north;
  - an alpine lake in the center-left;
  - a river canyon from the peaks, with a waterfall and a bridge;
  - a village with a wheat field;
  - a quarry, a radio hill and castle ruins;
  - a beach camp on the west coast, an island camp and a lighthouse in the south;
  - 11 dirt roads and trails, flattened and banked into the terrain, with fords where they cross rivers;
  - pine forests (about 28 000 trees), rocks and blockouts at the POIs.
- **How it is built:**
  - The level is a JSON definition, baked at load (about 270 ms) into a height grid at 1 m with a surface per cell:
    packed dirt, grass, rock, sand, mud and the new snow.
  - Physics: Jolt `HeightFieldShape` tiles of 255 m, streamed on a worker thread. Tree trunks and rocks are
    colliders.
  - Rendering: a terrain LOD over the same height grid (render and collision come from the same data), tree LODs
    with distant cards, longer shadow cascades, and a render-only horizon of hills and open sea around the map.
- **Water is render-only.** Rivers, the lake and the sea are surfaces drawn over rigid ground, with no buoyancy
  or drag. You can drive through the fords. If the vehicle stays in deep water for 1 s, it is put back on the
  nearest road with a message saying so. Near the edge of the map a warning appears; driving on puts the vehicle
  back on the nearest road. On a level, R resets to the nearest road.
- **Map (M):** a shaded overview with roads, rivers and the POIs. Click a POI or use the list to teleport.
- **Frame rate** (2880 × 1800, Metal, M-series Mac, display capped at 120 FPS):
  - High: 112–120 FPS on the roads, in the village and at the quarry, and 95 FPS looking across the lake;
  - Ultra in the village: 89 FPS;
  - Low and Medium: 120 FPS.
- **Streaming hitches:** crossing a tile border at 46 km/h costs 0.1 ms on the main thread. Tile builds run on the
  worker thread and take up to 7 ms each.
- Both vehicles (gasoline SUV and EV) were driven on the level in tests: spawn, road drive, ford, deep-water
  recovery, and parking at all 13 POIs.
- Smaller changes since v0.11.0:
  - EV traction control is on by default;
  - the gamepad diagram on the Controls page no longer overlaps its callouts.

## How to open it

**macOS.** The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.12.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

**Windows (untested on real hardware).** Unzip, keep the `assets` folder next to `trailforge.exe`, run it. The exe
is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn ("More info" → "Run anyway"). Needs a Direct3D 12 or Vulkan GPU driver.

**Linux (untested on real hardware).** `tar xzf TrailForge-0.12.0-linux-x64.tar.gz`, then
`./TrailForge-0.12.0-linux-x64/trailforge`. Needs a Vulkan driver (Mesa RADV / ANV / NVK or the NVIDIA driver).

## Known limitations

- Windows and Linux have never been run on real hardware; the Direct3D 12 path has not been run at all.
- Water has no physics: no buoyancy, drag, splash forces or engine stall. Deep water and the map edge are handled by
  putting the vehicle back on a road. Snow is rigid ground with low grip, and the ground does not deform.
- The terrain is procedural, with procedural materials. PBR ground textures are planned for v0.13.0.
- The horizon ring and the open sea outside the map are low-detail scenery you cannot reach.
- High drops to about 95 FPS when looking across the lake.
- From very high up, the sea inside the map is slightly lighter than the sea outside it.
- The level visuals were judged from captures by the developer agent, not by a person. No gamepad was connected
  during development.
- macOS build not notarized; Windows build not code-signed.
- Earlier limitations still apply:
  - grass, spray and body mud are render-only;
  - ABS and TC are simplified logic;
  - sand and mud are lower-friction rigid surfaces;
  - the SUV's 4H / 4L center lock is a strong limited-slip setting;
  - sounds were judged from capture analysis, not by ear.
