TrailForge v0.18.0, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine (SDL3 + SDL GPU, Jolt Physics
`WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio). This stage is the first part of the **world
look** work on Exploration Peaks: real CC0 models for trees, plants, rocks and cliffs, a reshaped canyon and animated
waterfalls.

**Platforms:**

| Package | Status |
|---|---|
| `TrailForge-0.18.0-macos-arm64.zip` | macOS 27 on Apple Silicon. **Run and verified** (built for macOS 27.0). |
| `TrailForge-0.18.0-windows-x64.zip` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (MSVC, x64); the D3D12 / Vulkan rendering has never been run. |
| `TrailForge-0.18.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (gcc, Ubuntu); the app was only started on Mesa lavapipe (software Vulkan) under Xvfb, without loading the level. |

## What is new

- **CC0 level models.** Fir trees, fir saplings, two broadleaf trees, shrubs, ferns, boulders, mossy rocks, two rock
  faces, a dead trunk, a stump and dry branches from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com) (credits in
  `assets/models/CREDITS.md`), converted to mesh LODs, collision meshes, tree impostors and BC7 textures. Trees pick
  a species per site (broadleaf near water, the village and on low ground). Tree crowns are built from clusters of
  the original foliage, so they keep their full shape at a game-sized triangle count.
- **Collision from the models.** Rocks, cliff faces, logs and stumps collide with their model's collision mesh. Tree
  trunks collide as a prism along the trunk. Bushes, ferns and branches are render-only; you drive through them.
- **Canyon.** The canyon walls no longer show vertical stripes or a saw-tooth rim: the carved river and lake banks
  are smoothed (the stripes were creases between neighbouring river segments). The Falls River canyon walls step
  down in rock ledges. Rock faces clad the steep walls, tilted into the slope (about 280 of them).
- **Waterfalls.** Every step of a river of 2 m or more gets a falling water sheet on a ballistic path, with streaks
  that run down at the speed of the falling water, foam on the pool and rising mist. They are render-only: there is
  no water flow simulation.
- **Performance** (2880 × 1800, five views: village, forest, lake, canyon, peaks). Low, Medium and High held 60 fps
  at every view in the last measurement. Ultra drops to 37–48 fps in the forest. The display caps the frame rate at
  60 fps, so lower GPU times cannot be measured.

## How to open it

**macOS.** The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.18.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

**Windows (untested on real hardware).** Unzip, keep the `assets` folder next to `trailforge.exe`, run it. The exe
is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn ("More info" → "Run anyway"). Needs a Direct3D 12 or Vulkan GPU driver.

**Linux (untested on real hardware).** `tar xzf TrailForge-0.18.0-linux-x64.tar.gz`, then
`./TrailForge-0.18.0-linux-x64/trailforge`. Needs a Vulkan driver (Mesa RADV / ANV / NVK or the NVIDIA driver).

## Known limitations

- Ultra misses 60 fps in the dense forest (37–48 fps). In one of three measurements High also dropped to 49 fps
  there; that did not repeat.
- Seen from above, a waterfall sheet still reads as a flat white panel. The mist does not fade against nearby
  objects, only against the ground.
- The forest floor is lighter than before: the slimmer real fir trees cast less shadow than the old cone trees.
- Water, sky and post effects (TAA, ambient occlusion, depth of field, motion blur, auto exposure) are unchanged in
  this version.
- The download is larger: the models add 84 MB.
- Nobody has judged the new world by eye beyond still captures, and not in motion.
- Windows and Linux have never been run on real hardware; the Direct3D 12 path has not been run at all.
- macOS build not notarized; Windows build not code-signed.
- Earlier limitations still apply:
  - water has no physics; deep water and the map edge put the vehicle back on a road;
  - snow, sand and mud are rigid surfaces with lower grip;
  - grass, ground cover, spray and body mud are render-only;
  - ABS and TC are simplified logic;
  - sounds were judged from capture analysis, not by ear;
  - nobody has driven with a real gamepad.
