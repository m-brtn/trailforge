First packaged build of TrailForge, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine
(SDL3 + SDL GPU on Metal, Jolt Physics `WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio).

**Platform:** macOS 27 on Apple Silicon (arm64) only. The binary is built for macOS 27.0
(`LSMinimumSystemVersion` 27.0); nothing else has been run.

## What is in this build

- Proving ground (~100 × 100 m): bumps, diagonal twister (wheel lift), low-grip patch, 12/20/28/35° hills,
  18° side slope, 0.15/0.30/0.45 m steps, rock field, concrete skid strip, and a mud & puddles meadow.
  Teleport points are in F1 → Tuning.
- 2000 kg 4×4 with independent virtual suspension (0.40 m travel), 5-speed automatic, low range ×2.72,
  open front / LSD rear and center differentials. The track width is now 1.80 m so the tires stick out past
  the fenders.
- Suspension made easy to see: per-wheel compression bars in the HUD ("air" when a wheel has no contact, red on the
  bump stop) and a lower follow camera.
- **Reverse is automatic:** hold S to brake, and keep holding it once the vehicle stops to engage reverse
  (S then drives backwards). In reverse it works the other way round: W brakes, then engages drive. Q still
  toggles the direction by hand when the vehicle is stopped. A driving read-out at the bottom of the screen
  shows D / R / N, speed, gear, HIGH / LOW range and rpm, plus short hints ("Reverse engaged",
  "Stop to change direction").
- Rendering: PBR, cascaded shadows, time of day (F2 → Look) with sun, moon and headlights, fog, bloom,
  instanced grass and pines, puddle reflections, visual-only tire marks and cosmetic mud.
- Recorded sound (55 Freesound files, credits in `TrailForge.app/Contents/Resources/assets/audio/CREDITS.md`)
  driven by engine rpm, wheel contacts and impacts.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Throttle / brake (hold S at a stop: reverse) | W / S | RT / LT |
| Steering | A / D | left stick |
| Handbrake (holds the vehicle on a slope) | Space | A |
| Forward ↔ reverse by hand (when stopped) | Q | X |
| Low range (when stopped) | L | Y |
| Reset to the start point | R | Back |
| Follow / free camera | C | RB |
| Headlights | H | — |
| Diagnostics / graphics / audio panels | F1 / F2 / F3 | — |
| Pause / single physics step | Esc / N | Start |
| Orbit / zoom camera | right mouse + drag / wheel | — |

## How to open it

The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.1.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

## Known limitations

- macOS 27 / Apple Silicon only; Metal only. Not notarized.
- The ground is rigid. Mud is a low-friction surface, puddles have no water physics, tire marks and vehicle dirt
  are visual only. No damage, no aerodynamics, no rolling resistance (a released vehicle rolls a little).
- With auto reverse, holding S on a slope backs the vehicle down after it stops. Use the handbrake to hold it
  (measured: drift under 1.3 cm in 2 s on 10–23° slopes of the hills), or turn auto reverse off in
  F1 → Tuning → Simulation.
- Keyboard pedals are on/off (smoothed); gamepad support is implemented but has not been tested with a real
  controller. Handling and sound have not been judged by hand / by ear yet; the checks are automated
  (scenario scripts and measurements).
- The 0.45 m step is borderline even in low range; cresting the 35° hill at full throttle can roll the vehicle.
