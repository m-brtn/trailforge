TrailForge v0.13.0, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine (SDL3 + SDL GPU, Jolt Physics
`WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio). This stage gives the ground of
**Exploration Peaks** real photo-scanned PBR materials (CC0, Poly Haven) at up to 4K.

**Platforms:**

| Package | Status |
|---|---|
| `TrailForge-0.13.0-macos-arm64.zip` | macOS 27 on Apple Silicon. **Run and verified** (built for macOS 27.0). |
| `TrailForge-0.13.0-windows-x64.zip` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (MSVC, x64); the D3D12 / Vulkan rendering has never been run. |
| `TrailForge-0.13.0-linux-x64.tar.gz` | **UNTESTED ON REAL HARDWARE.** Built and headless-tested in GitHub Actions (gcc, Ubuntu); the app was only started on Mesa lavapipe (software Vulkan) under Xvfb, without loading the level. |

## What is new

- **Terrain textures.** Six CC0 materials from Poly Haven: grass / forest floor, soil, gravel road, rock, beach sand
  and snow. Each has an albedo with a height map (4096²) and a normal / roughness / AO map (2048²), stored as BC7
  KTX2 with full mip chains (167.8 MB in total). Credits: `assets/textures/CREDITS.md`.
- **Blending.** Layers meet by their height maps (stones of the road poke through the grass edge, snow fills the
  hollows of the rock first). Rock is projected from three sides on steep faces. A second, rotated sample hides the
  repeats beyond 15 m; far away each layer fades to its average colour, so no tile pattern shows from the hills.
  The mud near water, wet banks, the wheat field and the distant grass colour are kept from the earlier material.
- **Quality.** Graphics → "Level ground textures": Off, 512, 1024, 2048, 4096. The presets use Low 512,
  Medium 1024, High 2048, Ultra 4096. Off (or a GPU without BC7, or missing files) uses the earlier procedural
  ground. The proving ground keeps its procedural materials.
- **Cost** (2880 × 1800, Metal, M-series Mac, vsync off, village view, textured vs Off in the same run):
  - Low 208 vs 215 FPS, Medium 172 vs 191 FPS, High 128 vs 136 FPS, Ultra 98 vs 100 FPS;
  - GPU time per frame +0.3 to +0.9 ms.
- **Loading, no streaming hitches while driving.** The textures load when you enter the level or change the size:
  Ultra 167.8 MB in about 0.15–0.17 s, High 67 MB in 0.02–0.09 s, Medium and Low under 0.03 s. Driving across the
  map loads nothing (checked across a physics tile border).
- **Reproducible assets.** `tools/textures/fetch_textures.py` downloads the sources, checks them against recorded
  hashes and re-encodes all maps (deterministic, 16 s on the Mac with the downloads cached). The repository stores
  the textures in Git LFS.
- Packaging: the Windows and Linux packages now also contain every asset folder, and all three packages check for
  the level and the textures.

## How to open it

**macOS.** The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.13.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

**Windows (untested on real hardware).** Unzip, keep the `assets` folder next to `trailforge.exe`, run it. The exe
is not code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn ("More info" → "Run anyway"). Needs a Direct3D 12 or Vulkan GPU driver.

**Linux (untested on real hardware).** `tar xzf TrailForge-0.13.0-linux-x64.tar.gz`, then
`./TrailForge-0.13.0-linux-x64/trailforge`. Needs a Vulkan driver (Mesa RADV / ANV / NVK or the NVIDIA driver).

## Known limitations

- Windows and Linux have never been run on real hardware; the Direct3D 12 path has not been run at all. BC7
  textures were only run on Metal.
- The textures are visual only: the ground stays rigid, and grip comes from the surface type of each spot, not from
  the texture.
- Textures load in one go when you enter the level (up to about 0.17 s on Ultra); there is no texture streaming.
- Seen from a free camera hundreds of metres up, the grass blades under the camera make a fine pattern on the
  ground. This does not happen from the chase camera.
- On meadows the soil shows through thin grass as light patches that repeat a little.
- Props, POI blockouts, trees, rocks, the vehicles and the proving ground keep their procedural materials.
- The look was judged from captures by the developer agent, not by a person. No gamepad was connected during
  development.
- macOS build not notarized; Windows build not code-signed.
- Earlier limitations still apply:
  - water has no physics; deep water and the map edge put the vehicle back on a road;
  - snow, sand and mud are rigid surfaces with lower grip;
  - grass, spray and body mud are render-only;
  - ABS and TC are simplified logic;
  - sounds were judged from capture analysis, not by ear.
