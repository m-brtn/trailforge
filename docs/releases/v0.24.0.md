TrailForge v0.24.0, an off-road vehicle prototype on a small custom C++20 engine (SDL3 + SDL GPU, Jolt Physics
`WheeledVehicleController`, Dear ImGui, own audio mixer on miniaudio). This stage shortens the braking distances
and polishes Exploration Peaks: the map edge, the Falls Pool, the castle ruins.

**Platforms:**

| Package | Status |
|---|---|
| `TrailForge-0.24.0-macos-arm64.zip` | macOS 27 on Apple Silicon. **Run and verified** (built for macOS 27.0). |

There are no Windows or Linux packages for this release: GitHub Actions did not run for these commits (the jobs
were not started because of an account billing limit), so nothing was built or tested on those platforms. Windows
and Linux have never been run on real hardware in any release.

## What is new

- **Shorter, honest stops.** A 100–0 km/h stop with ABS took about 46 m. Four causes were measured: the foot brake
  needed 0.33 s to reach full pressure, the SUV's front brakes were too weak while the rears locked first, the ABS
  dropped the pressure to zero and rebuilt it slowly, and the tire grip. Now the foot brake reaches full pressure in
  0.125 s, the ABS goes back quickly to 85 % of the pressure where the wheel last broke away, the brakes are
  front-biased (SUV 4200 / 2200 N·m, EV 4800 / 2400 N·m per wheel) and the EV's all-terrain tires have a little more
  grip under braking. No extra forces are added.
  Measured with the probe (100–0 km/h, ABS on): SUV 46.42 → 42.85 m, EV 46.37 → 39.92 m. 50–0: SUV 13.49 → 11.97 m,
  EV 13.88 → 11.56 m. Without ABS the stops are a few metres longer than before, because the front wheels now lock
  first. Mini track autopilot laps: SUV 0:59.69, EV 1:00.83.
- **Map edge blends into the horizon.** From above, the edge of the 2 × 2 km playable area read as a square: hard
  colours, streaks and a see-through sea rectangle. The distant hills now start in the colours of the ground at the
  edge and change over to their own grass / rock / snow / sand pattern; heights at the edge are smoothed, the sea
  outside is opaque, and trees thin out toward the edge. Render-only: nothing outside the playable area has
  collision, as before.
- **Falls Pool.** Dark and moss-coloured "shards" floated over the pool below the waterfall. They were the open
  backs of cliff-face rock models standing off a rim. Cliff faces are now placed only where their back stays inside
  the wall (fewer faces on the walls: 237 instead of 519), and ground cover never hangs over water.
- **Castle ruins on their marker.** The ruins were built about 35 m away from their map marker, because the marker
  sat on a road junction. The marker moved to free ground and the ruins stand on it.
- **Boathouse.** Its floor is at the lake surface (70.02 m, lake 70.00 m); a test now keeps it that way.

## How to open it

**macOS.** The app is **ad-hoc signed, not notarized**, so Gatekeeper blocks a plain double-click after downloading.

1. Unzip `TrailForge-0.24.0-macos-arm64.zip`.
2. Either right-click `TrailForge.app` → **Open** → **Open**, or remove the quarantine flag in Terminal:
   `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`
3. Optional command-line options (run the binary inside the bundle):
   `TrailForge.app/Contents/MacOS/trailforge --help`

## Known limitations

- From very high up the square of the playable area can still be seen: the ground inside has more contrast than the
  distant hills, and a soft snow band shows to the north-west.
- The SUV's 42.85 m stop is limited by its mud-terrain tires on the rigid concrete, not by the brakes.
- Brakes are a torque per wheel: no hydraulics, pad fade or temperature.
- The changes were judged on still captures and probe runs only; nobody drove them by eye.
- No Windows or Linux packages for this release; those platforms have never been run on real hardware.
- macOS build not notarized.
- Earlier limitations still apply:
  - water has no physics; deep water and the map edge put the vehicle back on a road;
  - snow, sand and mud are rigid surfaces with lower grip;
  - grass, ground cover, spray and body mud are render-only;
  - ABS and TC are simplified logic;
  - sounds were judged from capture analysis, not by ear;
  - nobody has driven with a real gamepad;
  - buildings have no interiors; Blender bodies are a test path only.
