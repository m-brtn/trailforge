# TrailForge v0.32.0

A localized woodland and rocky creek landscape in Exploration Peaks, upstream of the Valley Road ford.

## Changes

- A 300 m wide woodland zone centered at world coordinates (-230, -440), with a gradual outer transition:
  taller, broader tree crowns, a separate sapling layer, more shrubs/ferns and bank stones.
- Four irregular terrain benches along Glacier Creek, dressed with the existing CC0 rock models.
  Terrain, roads and rivers are baked together; rocks use their model collision meshes.
- Rock-wall shading retains scanned fractures and subtle neutral weathering instead of high-contrast
  procedural horizontal bands. This material adjustment applies to terrain rock walls throughout the map.
- Data-driven `woodlands` and `terraces` fields for procedural levels, plus a reproducible
  `tools/scenarios/woodland_views.txt` inspection/capture scenario.
- Existing models and textures are reused: no added LFS assets. Vehicle geometry and physics are unchanged.

## Verification

- Debug and Release builds and CTest: 114/114 passed in each configuration.
- Full Release gameplay regression: 248 passed, 0 failed (Low graphics / 50% render scale;
  all original assertions unchanged). Woodland visuals are captured separately at Ultra.
- Woodland scenario: 8/8 passed, including entering/exiting the ford, stopping on the road and no recoveries.

## Controls and location

W/S throttle and brake, A/D steering, Space handbrake, R reset, C camera, Esc pause.
M opens the map. Gamepad: triggers for pedals, left stick for steering, RB/R1 for camera.
Select Exploration Peaks and DETAILED trees in Settings. Follow Valley Road to its ford north of Alpine Lake;
this landscape extends upstream to the north. The capture scenario starts at the ford.

## Opening the app

Unzip `TrailForge-0.32.0-macos-arm64.zip`, then right-click `TrailForge.app` and choose Open.
The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. If needed:
`xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine TrailForge.app`.

## Known limitations

- This is one localized environment study, not a complete map overhaul or a claim of matching the reference image.
- The dense woodland increases GPU cost. Ultra at native Retina resolution is not a 60 FPS target on the tested Mac.
- Water remains a still rendered surface with vehicle buoyancy/drag; no flowing-water simulation was added.
  Terrain is rigid. Ground cover is visual; tree trunks and rock models have collision.
- macOS / Apple Silicon / Metal is the verified release platform. This release has no Windows or Linux packages:
  CI is paused. Windows and Linux have never been run on real hardware.
