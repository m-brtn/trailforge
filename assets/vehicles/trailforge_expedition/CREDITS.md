# TrailForge Expedition body

Original geometry authored for TrailForge with `tools/cars/blender/expedition_clay.py`,
following the user-provided expedition SUV design reference. No third-party mesh or texture is included.
Materials use scalar PBR values; Blender procedural noise is not exported.
Road wheels, spare tires and suspension are generated by TrailForge, not embedded in this asset.

Rebuild (macOS, Blender installed):

```sh
./build/release/trailforge_model_export assets/vehicles/trailforge_suv/vehicle.json /tmp/trailforge_suv_reference.obj
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b --python tools/cars/blender/expedition_clay.py -- --paint --no-render
/Applications/Blender.app/Contents/MacOS/Blender -b /tmp/expedition_paint.blend --python tools/cars/blender/export_expedition.py
```

The exporter maps material roles, preserves the reviewed body placement,
creates convex collision pieces and procedural spare seats, and invokes `trailforge_vehiclec`.
Wheel dimensions and steering retain the SUV values. Expedition uses 0.34-0.55 m suspension limits,
a 35 mm bump-stop zone and a raise-only 0-80 mm garage range; there is no visual body lift.
