## Goal of the day

Build a runnable TrailForge prototype — a 3D off-road game on a small custom engine. Top priority is
convincing off-road vehicle behavior: mass, suspension, wheel spin, lifted wheels, rocks and climbs.

- Stack: C++20, CMake + Ninja, CMakePresets (Debug/Release, plus headless), SDL3, SDL GPU (Metal),
  Jolt Physics (`VehicleConstraint` + `WheeledVehicleController`), Dear ImGui, GLM.

- Procedural geometry, no external assets. Test ground ~100×100 m. Headless physics tests in CTest.

## Problems and solutions

- **No cmake and ninja.** Installed via Homebrew.
- **Crash on startup (SIGSEGV), first seen in the settle test.** Debugged with lldb.
  Cause: `SurfaceTable` created Jolt objects (`PhysicsMaterialSimple`) before the Jolt allocator was
  registered — the class member was constructed before `PhysicsWorld`. Fix: a separate RAII object `JoltRuntime`,
  placed as the first member of `Simulation`, which initializes Jolt before everything else.
- **No screen and input access for Computer Use** (Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions not granted).
  Visual verification was done with the built-in flag `--capture file.bmp [--capture-at N]`, which saves the
  rendered frame.
- **Minor issues while debugging scenarios:** the vehicle drove off the edge of the start pad in tests (the pad
  was widened); at the edge of the test ground the vehicle flew over the berm (barrier reworked: berm plus concrete walls).
- **(~20:00) UI forces 1/N too low with collision steps N > 1.** Cause: Jolt lambdas are impulses of the last
  collision substep, but were divided by the full step. Fix: `postPhysics(dt / collisionSteps)`.
- **(~20:00) Brake could not stop the vehicle in low range.** Cause: Jolt's automatic keeps the clutch engaged in gear;
  idle engine torque through the clutch (~840 N·m) beat the brakes. Fix: with brake and no throttle the transmission
  is switched to manual — neutral near standstill, current gear (no shifts) while moving.
  Low range stop 10.4 m/s → 0 in 1.33 s (was 1.99 s); flat brake test 16.1 → 8.1 m/s in 1 s (was 8.3).
- **Synthetic key events were not seen as held keys.** Cause: `Input` read `SDL_GetKeyboardState`, which
  `SDL_PushEvent` does not update. Fix: held state tracked from KEY_DOWN/KEY_UP events.
- **Steps teleport point sat on the side-slope run-out**, the vehicle drifted off line. Moved to z = −7.5.
- **ImGui number entry on macOS** is Cmd+click (Ctrl+click emulates right click in macOS mode).
- **(~20:35) Scenario Rebuild click missed (3 FAILs at lines 451/453/455, 2 of 2 runs).** Cause: `scrollto` finished
  as soon as the widget rect was inside the Tuning window, but ImGui applied one more queued wheel step a frame later
  (~65 pt scroll); the click landed 67 pt below the button (y = 850 vs actual 784). Fix: `scrollto` also requires the
  widget position to stay unchanged for 4 consecutive frames. This is the "Rebuild click missed" flake noted at ~20:26.
- **(~20:30) One run stalled after `teleport 7`** (rocks section, 4 cascaded FAILs: no movement, the next teleport was
  not applied either). Not reproduced in the next 3 runs; not investigated further.

## Results

Everything below was actually run on macOS 27 / Apple Silicon.

- **Build:** Debug, Release and headless presets build without errors; the metallib is embedded in the binary.
- **CTest: 6 of 6 tests pass:**
  - suspension settle — total load 19631 N vs weight m·g = 19620 N, no NaN;
  - throttle — 0 → 16.2 m/s in 4 s;
  - braking — to a full stop;
  - a wheel hanging over a pit loses contact and load (0 N), the others carry the weight;
  - reset restores state (gear, RPM, pose);
  - identical result with different frame schedules (at the same step rate).
- **Performance:** physics step ~0.035 ms at 120 Hz; app ~120 FPS on Metal (VSync).
- **Demo turn at 40 km/h:** body roll 8.5°, outer wheels compressed 77–79%.
- **(~20:26) CTest 8 of 8 in Debug and Release.** New: `corner_load_balance` (mean Σload 19620 N = m·g at
  1/2/4 collision steps), `brake_holds_in_low_range` (stop in 1.33 s, speed never rises, final 0 m/s).
- **(~20:26) Scenario `tools/scenarios/full_check.txt`: 128 checks, 0 failed** — two consecutive Release runs and one
  Debug run (~110 s each). Covers throttle/brake, steering and return, Q/L refused while moving and working at
  standstill, reverse, handbrake, R, C + free camera, F1, Esc pause (no ticks) and N = exactly one tick, ImGui input
  capture (W/R ignored while a field is focused, slider value applied), hills 12/20/28/35° in low range with brake
  hold < 5 cm, side slope, diagonal wheel lift, steps 0.15/0.30/0.45 m, rocks, low-grip patch, low camera orbit
  (clearance > 0.1 m), `SDL_SetWindowSize` to 900×560 / 320×200 / 1600×1000 / 1440×900 (depth texture recreated
  and matching the swapchain each time), Rebuild with mass 2600 kg. Frames: `/tmp/trailforge_01_start.png` …
  `/tmp/trailforge_22_rebuilt.png`.
- **Load anomaly explained.** Flat steady turn: mean Σload = m·g. On the demo track 1 s windows reach 113–130 % of m·g,
  while Σ(F·ŷ) equals m(g + a_y) exactly: load is along tilted contact normals, includes vertical accelerations
  and bump-stop hits (peaks up to ~330 kN). Physics, not a scaling error.
- **(~20:39) Before the MVP commit:** `cmake --preset debug/release` + build + `ctest`: 8 of 8 in Debug and
  Release (also after the `scrollto` fix). Release `--script tools/scenarios/full_check.txt`: 121/7 (stall after
  teleport 7 + Rebuild miss), 125/3 (Rebuild miss), then with the fix **128 passed, 0 failed in 2 of 2 runs** (~105 s each).
  The scenario was not re-run in Debug after the `scrollto` fix.
