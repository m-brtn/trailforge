# Devlog — 2026-10-24 (Saturday)

## Goal of the day

Build a runnable TrailForge prototype — a 3D off-road game on a small custom engine. Top priority is
convincing off-road vehicle behavior: mass, suspension, wheel spin, lifted wheels, rocks and climbs.

- Stack: C++20, CMake + Ninja, CMakePresets (Debug/Release, plus headless), SDL3, SDL GPU (Metal),
  Jolt Physics (`VehicleConstraint` + `WheeledVehicleController`), Dear ImGui, GLM.
- Dependencies via FetchContent with pinned versions.
- Procedural geometry, no external assets. Test ground ~100×100 m. Headless physics tests in CTest.

## Problems and solutions

- **No cmake and ninja.** Installed via Homebrew.
- **Crash on startup (SIGSEGV), first seen in the settle test.** Debugged with lldb.
  Cause: `SurfaceTable` created Jolt objects (`PhysicsMaterialSimple`) before the Jolt allocator was
  registered — the class member was constructed before `PhysicsWorld`. Fix: a separate RAII object `JoltRuntime`,
  placed as the first member of `Simulation`, which initializes Jolt before everything else.
- **No screen and input access for Computer Use** (Accessibility and Screen Recording permissions not granted).
  Visual verification was done with the built-in flag `--capture file.bmp [--capture-at N]`, which saves the
  rendered frame.
- **Minor issues while debugging scenarios:** the vehicle drove off the edge of the start pad in tests (the pad
  was widened); at the edge of the test ground the vehicle flew over the berm (barrier reworked: berm plus concrete walls).
- **(~20:00) UI forces 1/N too low with collision steps N > 1.** Cause: Jolt lambdas are impulses of the last
  collision substep, but were divided by the full step. Fix: `postPhysics(dt / collisionSteps)`.
- **(~20:00) Brake could not stop the vehicle in low range.** Cause: Jolt's automatic keeps the clutch engaged in gear;
  idle engine torque through the clutch (~840 N·m) beat the brakes. Fix: with brake and no throttle the transmission
  is switched to manual — neutral near standstill, current gear (no shifts) while moving.
  Low range stop 10.4 m/s → 0 in 1.33 s (was 1.99 s); flat brake test 16.1 → 8.1 m/s in 1 s (was 8.3).
- **Synthetic key events were not seen as held keys.** Cause: `Input` read `SDL_GetKeyboardState`, which
  `SDL_PushEvent` does not update. Fix: held state tracked from KEY_DOWN/KEY_UP events.
- **Steps teleport point sat on the side-slope run-out**, the vehicle drifted off line. Moved to z = −7.5.
- **ImGui number entry on macOS** is Cmd+click (Ctrl+click emulates right click in macOS mode).
- **(~20:35) Scenario Rebuild click missed (3 FAILs at lines 451/453/455, 2 of 2 runs).** Cause: `scrollto` finished
  as soon as the widget rect was inside the Tuning window, but ImGui applied one more queued wheel step a frame later
  (~65 pt scroll); the click landed 67 pt below the button (y = 850 vs actual 784). Fix: `scrollto` also requires the
  widget position to stay unchanged for 4 consecutive frames. This is the "Rebuild click missed" flake noted at ~20:26.
- **(~20:30) One run stalled after `teleport 7`** (rocks section, 4 cascaded FAILs: no movement, the next teleport was
  not applied either). Not reproduced in the next 3 runs; not investigated further.
- **(visual stage) Washed-out, foggy image.** Cause: tonemap input too bright, fog and ambient too strong.
  Fix: ACES pre-exposure 0.6, lower fog density, ambient ×0.55, darker grass/rock/mud albedo, bluer sky zenith.
- **Blocky mud borders** along the 0.5 m triangle grid. Cause: material chosen per triangle. Fix: dirt/grass/mud
  blended in the shader from the ground mask (R grass, G mud, B wet) plus noise.
- **Mud band in the low ground at z ≈ 46–48.** Cause: the mud mask used a height-based rim. Fix: mask G = blurred
  (0.75 m box) physics `Mud` indicator, so the look follows the actual low-mu surface.
- **Noisy puddles / white wet-mud sheen.** Fix: lower-frequency, weaker ripples; wet roughness 0.38–0.40,
  ground specular occlusion 0.75.
- **Vehicle dirt looked like camouflage.** Fix: splash line rising from the bottom of the body with streak noise;
  radial mapping on the wheels.
- **Debug lines changed color after tonemapping.** Fix: line colors are pre-inverted through ACES/exposure.
- **First perf samples were misleading** (Low at 60 FPS with 16.5 ms GPU, High at 270 FPS). Cause: the VSync switch
  and GPU clock ramp-up had not settled. Fix: 4 s warm-up in `perf_presets.txt`. Remaining run-to-run noise is
  external (see "Limitations").
- **(time of day) Scenario rejected `time`.** Cause: `Scenario::load` validates op names. Fix: added to the known set.
- **(time of day) `expect sun_el` right after `time` read the previous value.** Cause: the renderer applies settings
  one frame later. Fix: `tod`/`sun_el`/`sun_az` read the pending `GraphicsState` settings.
- **(time of day) "Headlights off" capture still showed the beams.** Cause: `set headlights 1` ran in the same frame as
  the preceding `capture`, before it was rendered. Fix: a `wait` after the capture in the scenario.
- **(time of day) Perf baseline not comparable.** The display ran at 60 Hz in this session (VSync 58–59 FPS, GPU
  clocks lower) vs 120 Hz earlier. Fix: built `4826485` in a temporary worktree and ran both builds back-to-back.
- **(tire marks) Rubber marks while rolling in 2nd gear** (~224 concrete segments). Cause: the sim has a steady
  1.6–2.1 m/s patch slip (~12 %) at full throttle in 2nd gear, above the sliding-speed threshold. Fix: gate the slip
  mark by the slip ratio (sliding speed / max(patch speed, rolling speed, 1)). The 1st-gear launch keeps its marks:
  the wheels really spin at 3–4.5 m/s for ~2 s.
- **(tire marks) All captures identical.** Cause: `capture` does not yield, the next `camera` ran in the same frame.
  Fix: `wait 0.3` after each capture.
- **(tire marks) Grass marks too bright/white, mud ruts looked like gravel, rubber shiny at grazing angles, dirt marks
  lost in the vehicle shadow.** Fix: darker pressed grass and roughness 0.85; glossier rut floor with weaker,
  lower-frequency lugs and lumps; rubber roughness 0.75; dirt compaction ×0.72 and a Noon capture from behind.
- **(tire marks) Low-range donut rolled the vehicle over** (binary keys, full lock). Dropped from the scenario; the
  handbrake drift on the start pad is used instead. `tap L` while moving was refused — moved after the stop.
- Preview loops had seam discontinuities (up to 24x the typical step) -> rebuilt loops with crossfades; engine loop length searched for the best seam correlation.
- Linear crossfades dipped up to ~2 dB on high-RPM loops -> constant-power crossfade weights for the measured segment correlation.
- Transient textures could not reach -18 LUFS under -1 dBTP -> circular look-ahead limiter with at most 6 dB reduction.
- A 30 s stereo menu pad cost 5.8 MB -> shortened to 18 s (24 beats at 80 bpm), 3.5 MB.
- **(audio) Tire layers saturated at 1.2 with four wheels on the same surface.** Cause: per-wheel intensities were
  power-summed. Fix: RMS over the four wheels (one wheel alone gives half the level of all four).
- **(audio) Engine pitch did not follow rpm for the first ~1.3 s of the launch** (spectrogram: falling 45 Hz line
  while rpm rose 800 → 3400; only 76 % of windows within 5 %). Cause: the starter recording keeps idling after the
  catch and the loops were still faded in by file time (2.3–4.3 s × pitch). Fix: the starter one-shot is tracked and
  crossfaded (equal power) against the loops; opening the throttle hands over at once. Result: 98–99 % of windows.
- **(audio) DC offset −0.005 in the mix.** Cause: exhaust-mic loops carry DC (idle loop −0.044). Fix: 10 Hz one-pole
  DC blocker before the limiter → −0.000002.
- **(audio) Jolt's `ContactListener` reports no impulse** (called before the solver). Body impact strength is an
  estimate from the approach speed and the effective mass at the contact point, in N·s (documented in README).
- **(audio) No numpy in the system Python** → analysis run from a venv (`/tmp/tfvenv`); the script needs numpy.
- **(audio) Scenario `expect audio_ev1 >= 2` at 30 km/h failed** (only one gear change yet) → moved to the brake point;
  `audio_day < 0.05` 4 s after `time Night` was 0.07 (ambience smoothing) → wait 5 s.

## Limitations and open questions

- **Not verified:** manual keyboard control, gamepad, window resize (being checked in the current stage).
  (~20:26) Still not verified by hand: real HID keyboard/mouse and macOS focus handling, gamepad (none connected),
  dragging the window edge (resize only via `SDL_SetWindowSize`), subjective handling feel. Reason: Accessibility and
  Screen Recording are not granted to Cursor.
- **Anomaly:** in a turn, the total wheel load exceeds the vehicle weight. Not investigated.
  (~20:26) Investigated and explained — see "Results".
- (~20:26) 0.45 m step (above the 0.4 m wheel radius): blocked in high range, climbed from standstill in low range,
  but borderline — one Debug run spun ~6 s before climbing. 35° hill: can roll over when cresting at full throttle.
  No rolling resistance → creeps a few cm after brake release.
- (~20:26) Flakes seen once each, not reproduced: Rebuild click missed; second C press lost. Suspect the real mouse
  cursor (ImGui SDL3 backend uses the global cursor position when the window is focused but not hovered).
- macOS only: shaders are written in MSL.
- Suspension is virtual (as in Jolt), ground is rigid and non-deformable.
- Trajectories differ at physics rates of 60 / 120 / 240 Hz (determinism only at the same rate).
- The project is not in git.
  (~20:39) It is in git now: `main` on https://github.com/m-brtn/game.git.
- (~20:39) The one-off stall after `teleport 7` is unexplained (possibly real mouse/keyboard focus; the real cursor
  sits outside the window and the ImGui backend reads its global position).
- (visual stage) **Perf numbers are noisy:** with VSync off the same preset measured 60–270 FPS between runs, frame
  counts quantized at 60/120 Hz. The machine had other load (VM, mediaanalysisd, WindowServer ~37 % CPU) and the
  window was not focused. One Ultra + VSync run averaged 51.4 FPS with a 316 ms hitch. With VSync on the rate was
  ~60 FPS in the perf runs but 116–120 FPS during `full_check`; the cause of the 60 vs 120 Hz difference is unknown.
- Mud is a rigid low-mu surface; puddles are dips in rigid ground with a render-only water plane; vehicle dirt is
  cosmetic. All puddles share one water level (single planar reflection).
- Visual vehicle parts stick out slightly beyond the three collision boxes.
- Shaders are MSL only; only macOS / Metal was run.
- Physics debug lines are now off by default (Tuning → Debug drawing).
- (time of day) The 5 m perimeter wall (collision) crosses every low view of the horizon; the banner composition
  is framed around it, not without it. Mountains are a sky-pass backdrop at infinity (no parallax, no collision).
- (time of day) Headlights are unshadowed and stylized (wrapped N·L, flat falloff to ~25 m); the moon is always
  full. Night exposure is raised (×2.7) for readability, so it is brighter than a real night.
- (time of day) The rock field rocks are small and mostly buried, so the facets read best on the showcase boulders.
  The rock collision shape changed (same positions and sizes); `full_check` rocks section still passes, but the
  handling feel over rocks was not compared.
- (time of day) Not checked: the time-lapse visually in motion (only its wrap via the scenario), dawn/dusk shadow
  swap sun → moon in motion, Medium/Low presets at night.
- (tire marks) Marks are visual only: no friction, height or collision change; relief only in the shading normal.
  The trample mask is 0.1 m per texel and refreshes every 0.5 s while marks fade. The 16k/64k perf numbers use a
  synthetic random fill, and GPU cost is whole-frame (the marks pass was not timed separately). A sustained donut is
  not achievable with binary keys; the handbrake drift is short. Marks were not checked in motion by eye, only in
  still captures.
- (audio assets) Engine is a petrol inline-4 (Mercedes 190E 16V), not a V8/diesel truck (recommend pitch down 15-25% and cap ~4500 rpm); engine_stop is from a different car; bottom-out uses trunk/door slams as proxies; no transmission whine (synthesize from driveshaft speed); tire_mud_slosh_loop is CC BY 4.0 (jymdavis) and needs attribution; ui_click (round 1) is brighter/louder than the menu family — use the new ui_* set; all assets come from lossy HQ previews; nothing listened to by ear.
- (audio) **Nobody listened to the mix.** All claims come from captures, CSV parameters and spectrograms. The device
  path was run (scenarios pass with it open), but the output was not heard.
- (audio) `engine_stop` is loaded but unused (there is no engine-off state in the sim). The whine is synthesized.
  Body impact impulse is estimated (see Problems). No Doppler, occlusion or reverb; listener = camera.
- (audio) Capture is deterministic for a given tick sequence, but scenario input is real-time, so two captures differ
  slightly (pitch-tracking 98 vs 99 % between runs). The capture clock lags the scenario clock when frames drop
  ticks (≈1 s over 50 s).
- (audio) Assets are found next to the binary or via the source-tree path compiled in; the `.app` bundle does not
  contain them yet. Device-open failure and missing assets are handled in code (log + silent run) but were not
