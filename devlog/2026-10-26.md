## Stages

Times are from the file modification times of the work (the stages before 00:38 were not timed).

  the chassis box still collided with it. Fixed the winding of all six faces; split two-box props (radio tower legs)

  rock, every 7th trunk wound outward) and `level_bridges` (both vehicles, both bridges, both directions on the
  wheels from 30 m before to 15 m past the deck).
- **~00:35 — Houses off the roads** (with the parallel level agent's placement work): buildings and props are pushed
  out of the road corridors; CTest `level_placement`.
- **00:38–00:41 — Hood camera and right stick.** `Camera::Mode::Hood` (C / RB cycles follow → hood → free), mount in
  body space from `camera.hood` in vehicle.json (validated by the loader), own near plane. Right stick: follow orbit
  with a recentre behind a moving vehicle 1.75 s after release, hood look-around that springs back; quadratic
  response after a 0.15 dead zone (`rightStickValue`). CTest `trailforge_camera_tests` (4 tests).
- **~00:40 — Controls declutter.** Menu navigation (Return / Backspace, pad A as MENU SELECT, B as MENU BACK) is no
  longer listed on the Controls page; the right stick is (CAMERA ORBIT / LOOK); unbound face buttons are not drawn.
- **01:07 — BACK at the top of every page.** `MenuUI::drawBackButton`: a "◀ BACK" box above the page title, top

  row hit test returning the draw index instead of the item index.
- **01:15 — Gamepad selection.** A real pad is connected to the test Mac and made controls.txt / camera.txt depend
  on it. Input now follows the newest connected pad and falls back to another on removal; checks use
  `controls pad_type none` and a virtual SDL pad (`gamepad attach|detach|axis`).
- **01:18 — No grass on bridges and roads.** Placement problem, not depth: blades were rooted on the terrain a few
  centimetres below the deck ramps, and road-edge grass leaked back over the road through the 3 × 3 mask blur. The
  bake now multiplies the grass and wheat mask after the blur by a keep factor
  `min(smoothstep(1.5, 2.7, road clearance), smoothstep(1.5, 3.0, bridge clearance))` (deck plus ramps);
  `roadClearance` (trees, rocks, props) also includes the bridge footprint. CTest `level_grass_clear`.
  exploration_check drives over the Coast and Canyon bridges with approach captures.
- **01:23 — Scenario clock.** Root cause of the 60 Hz differences: `until` is evaluated per frame and the input
  reaches the vehicle 3–4 frames later, ~67 ms at 60 Hz against ~33 ms at 120 Hz. During a scenario every frame now
  advances a fixed 1/120 s (paced with `SDL_DelayPrecise`, never faster than real time; real frame time for FPS and
  inside `perf_begin`..`perf_end`).
- **01:23–01:45 — Draw distances.** Settings → Graphics GRASS DISTANCE (PRESET, 30–250 m) and TREE DETAIL DISTANCE
  (PRESET, 250–2000 m), F2 sliders (grass, tree detail, tree cull), `grass_distance` / `tree_distance` in settings.cfg
  (0 = preset). Presets: grass 40 / 70 / 110 / 160 m (was 22 / 32 / 45 / 60), tree detail 380 / 500 / 650 / 900 m
  (mesh LOD ends were 55 / 140 / 380 m on every preset, now 0.145 / 0.37 / 1.0 of the detail distance), tree cull

- **~01:55 — Hood framing.** At [0, 1.24, 0.62] pitch −4° the hood filled 23% of the view (top edge at 77% of the

- **02:50 — Scenario start race.** tracks, suspension, audio_check, skid_check failed with the first `teleport`

## Problems and solutions

- **Wheels fall through the bridge deck.** Inverted slab winding; Jolt's wheel casts ignore back faces. Rewound the
  slab, added the winding audit test.
- **BACK click on the Controls page did nothing.** The hit test returned the index into the drawn rows. `Row` now
  carries the item index.
- **controls.txt / camera.txt failed with a real pad connected.** The input took the first pad. Newest pad wins,
  scenario checks pin the pad type or use a virtual pad.
- **Grass on bridge ramps and roads.** Terrain-rooted blades under the deck ramps and the blurred mask. Keep factor
  after the blur from the same road / bridge data.
- **Scenario results depended on the refresh rate.** Frame-counted latency. Fixed virtual step.
- **First `teleport` of a script ignored.** The start level load in the first tick dropped it (also in v0.13.0).
  Scripts wait for pending level loads.
- **Ultra grass 160 m too expensive.** The dense LOD radius scaled with the distance. Capped the dense LOD radii.

## Limitations and open questions

- The real gamepad on the test Mac was only used to find the pad-selection bug; nobody drove with it.
- Grass fade and the tree cross-fade were checked on still captures only, not in motion by eye.
- Rocks and props have one mesh each and no LOD system, so there is no "object detail distance" setting.
- The perf numbers carry background load (a VM, Spotlight); the village view varied by up to 2× between runs.
- Windows / Linux: CI build and headless tests only, never run on real hardware.
