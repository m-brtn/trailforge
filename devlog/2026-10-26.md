# Devlog — 2026-10-26 (Monday)

## Goal of the day

Stage v0.14.0 (branch `feature/bridge-camera`, worktree from 674b097): wheels must drive on the bridges, hood camera,
right-stick camera on the gamepad, a decluttered Controls page, houses off the roads, a BACK button at the top of
every menu page, scenario timing independent of the display refresh rate, no grass on bridges and roads, and
longer grass / tree draw distances with their own settings. Then release v0.14.0.

Stage v0.15.0 (branch `feature/traced-cars`, worktree `/tmp/tf-cars`): build both car bodies from the
`Cars.png` concept sheet, proportions and form first; collision from the same data; keep the suspension visible.
Then release v0.15.0 after v0.14.0.

Map and attract drive stage (branch `feature/map-autopilot`, worktree `/tmp/tf-map` from db41e91): behind the intro
and the start menu the vehicle drives a looped route on its own pedals and steering (autopilot, honest DriverIntent
only), followed by a cinematic camera; a full-screen map (M / pad LB / pause menu), a waypoint marker, a compass bar
and a corner minimap. Then the next release.

Buildings stage (branch `feature/buildings`, worktree `/tmp/tf-buildings` from origin/main): replace the blockout
village and POI boxes with real buildings from a modular procedural kit (Alpine houses, fuel station, workshop, castle
ruins, lighthouse, radio mast, quarry machinery, docks, camps, village extras), CC0 materials through
`fetch_textures.py`, collision from the same parts, LODs, the map, a `buildings_views.txt` scenario. Then the next
release.

Blender body import stage (branch `feature/vehicle-glb`, worktree `/tmp/tf-vglb` from b52b706): a game-side path for
Blender-authored vehicle bodies (`assets/vehicles/<id>/body.glb`): contract, converter with validation, loader and
render path, collision from `COL_*` pieces, headless Blender scripts, an end-to-end test asset. Then the next release.

## Problems and solutions

- **(world beauty) Level tests segfaulted on Windows only.** `Level::heightAt` clamped the grid coordinate to
  `float(m_n - 1) - 1e-4f`, which rounds back to 2560.0 for the 2561² grid, so `i + 1` read one sample past the end
  (harmless on macOS / Linux, a crash with MSVC). Found with an ASan + UBSan build. The cell index is now clamped as
  an integer to `m_n - 2`; ASan / UBSan clean on the level, core, vehicle and audio tests.

- **Wheels fall through the bridge deck.** Inverted slab winding; Jolt's wheel casts ignore back faces. Rewound the
  slab, added the winding audit test.
- **BACK click on the Controls page did nothing.** The hit test returned the index into the drawn rows. `Row` now
  carries the item index.
- **controls.txt / camera.txt failed with a real pad connected.** The input took the first pad. Newest pad wins,
  scenario checks pin the pad type or use a virtual pad.
- **Grass on bridge ramps and roads.** Terrain-rooted blades under the deck ramps and the blurred mask. Keep factor
  after the blur from the same road / bridge data.
- **Scenario results depended on the refresh rate.** Frame-counted latency. Fixed virtual step.
- **First `teleport` of a script ignored.** The start level load in the first tick dropped it (also in v0.13.0).
  Scripts wait for pending level loads.
- **Ultra grass 160 m too expensive.** The dense LOD radius scaled with the distance. Capped the dense LOD radii.
- **Exploded / stretched model geometry.** `json::write` printed large integers as `1.63958e+06`; cgltf reads integer
  fields (byteOffset) with `strtol`, so offsets became 1. `writeNumber` prints integral values as integers; all
  models regenerated.
- **Whole frame corrupted (black sky, garbage) once the models loaded.** BC7 mip levels narrower than 4 texels on one
  side (4 × 1, 2 × 1 of the 1024 × 256 impostor atlases) broke the Metal frame. The loader keeps only levels with both
  sides ≥ 4.
- **Overlapping windowed run.** One of my quick capture runs started while another agent's full_check (from
  /tmp/tf-cars) was running and overlapped it for about 3 minutes before I killed mine; their run may have been
  disturbed. Since then every windowed run goes through a wrapper that waits for `pgrep 'trailforge.*--script|package_macos'`.
- **Attract drive: the SUV left the route at the Valley Road / Lake Trail junction** (2H: off-route reset after
  421 m, 4H: stuck). Cause: an 85° corner where the legs meet, and right after it the speed target jumped back to
  cruise, so the PI controller went to full throttle while steering on a grade; in 2H the rear stepped out (power
  oversteer, countersteer). Fix: corners rounded in the route bake, an acceleration ramp in the speed profile, the
  throttle capped at 0.65 and reduced with steering.
- **M did not close the map.** Closing the map in `handleMap` switched the screen back to gameplay, and the gameplay
  branch of the same frame saw the same M press and opened it again. The frame that handled the map now skips the
  gameplay actions.
- **POI labels overlapped on the proving ground** (Mud & puddles, Puddle, Meadow). Labels go right, else left of the
  circle, and are left out when both would cover another label or circle (the number and the list still name it).

- **Tire flare inside the wheel sweep** (`model_wheel_clearance`). The fender cap disc covered the tire side. The
  fender is a lip open towards the tire.
- **Fainter tire marks** (`suv_marks_keyboard`, `launch_marks_proportional`). Shorter wheelbase and track of the
  concept. Thresholds lowered with the measured values in comments.
- **suspension.txt flaky** (wheel-in-the-air check). The traced bodies settle with all four wheels down at the old
  stop points. The check is now `susp_spread > 0.20` (new scenario variable; measured 0.291, 0.226 after the hood-line
  change).
- **Ridges across the hood.** Parts drawn above the hood in the side view, extruded across the width by the visual
  hull. Hood line and `fender_top` anchors as keypoint data.
- **Attract drive stuck after merging the traced cars (v0.15.0).** `autopilot_loops`: SUV 2H and EV stuck at the
  80° Valley Road / Lake Trail junction corner (radius ~8.5 m). Cause: pure pursuit with a 7–11 m lookahead cuts a
  corner by about L² / 8R, here 2.4 m onto the road-cut bank (30° tilt), and in 2H the climb stalled; the shorter
  wheelbase (2.80 → 2.56 / 2.44 m) and new inertia of the traced bodies turned the earlier 2.5 m scrape into a stall.
  Fix: in tight curves the lookahead is at most 0.6 × the curve radius (not below 3.5 m). Max off the line on
  Exploration Peaks 2.5 → 0.75 m, proving ground 3.6 → 2.0 m; the test bound is now 2.5 m (was 4 m).
- **Paint: the plan's shoulder colour on the greenhouse** (olive bands in the side windows). The plan view painted
  points below the roof edge it cannot see. Views now only paint points on their outermost surface (depth maps).
- **Paint: ragged roof edges.** The view switch followed the ripples of the traced outline (mesh normals, even over
  8 cm). The view is chosen with the field normal over 30 cm.
- **Captures looked unchanged between runs.** The image viewer cached files by path; every capture set now gets a
  new name.

- **(beauty post) Vertical stripes on the Falls Pool wall with AO on.** `ao_strength 6` showed them clearly; a
  horizon bias and snapping the AO samples to full-resolution texel centres did not help. The steep wall is the
  height grid, whose steps are concave corners a few cm deep; the first GTAO samples (1 px away) found them.
  Solution: no AO samples closer than 0.3 m. The stripes are gone at strength 6, the contact darkening at the wall
  foot stays.
- **(beauty post) Green sky near the horizon.** The flat single-scattering model reddens the sunlight over the full
  ground-level air mass and multiplies it with the blue in-scatter: green-yellow horizons at 8–30° sun. Solution:
  0.35 of the air mass for the sunlight (most scattering happens higher up) and an ozone absorption term.
- **(beauty post) Background jobs died.** Gate chains started with `nohup … &` from a tool shell were killed when
  that shell call ended (logs stopped mid-run). Solution: run them as tool-managed background shells.
- **(beauty post) Opaque bench passes cost nothing.** Repeating opaque fullscreen draws inside one render pass is
  removed by the tile GPU's hidden-surface removal; `post_bench` repeats whole render passes.
- **(buildings) See-through kit walls.** The model shader's `mm.params.x > 0.5 ? modelCoverage(a.a) : 1.0` evaluates
  both sides in HLSL, so the alpha cut (alpha < 0.5) ran on opaque materials too, and the kit albedos carried height
  in alpha. Fix without a shader change (another agent works on the shaders): `texc albedo` without `--height` writes
  alpha 1; `buildings.json` sets `"heightInAlpha": false` and `fetch_textures.py` then skips the displacement maps.
  `building_kit_materials` checks the flag.
- **(buildings) Blue, metallic corrugated walls.** `corrugated_iron` is a blue painted, metal 0.98 scan; replaced by
  `worn_corrugated_iron` (1.8 m tile). Painted metal (`metal_plate_02` is dark rust) is now an untextured tint.
- **(buildings) Render-only parts in reach.** `building_kit_parts` found far-LOD vertices up to 1.04 m off the
  collision below 6 m: pump heads, the shop roof unit, the lighthouse door and windows, the mast and dock ladder
  stiles (now solid pieces), decals on cylinder vertices instead of facet centers (ruin tower slits / door, dome tent
  door; now on facet centers). The ladder rungs stay render-only between the solid stiles.
- **(buildings) Buried and hanging houses after a placement move.** `settle` moved a whole group by the height
  change at its center, which buried a house's back 3.7 m into a slope; re-running the floor rule there left the
  front 1.6 m above the ground. Now every building on the ground gets its floor rule again at the new place, and a
  group only moves where the plinths of its structures still reach the ground (`level_buildings`).

- **Blender's convex hulls failed the convexity check (up to 90 mm).** The first check took the authored triangles'
  planes; Blender's `convex_hull` leaves sliver triangles (edges of a few mm next to 0.1 m ones) whose planes are
  tilted, so far points appear "in front". Solution: the check builds the hull of the piece's points with Jolt's
  `ConvexHullBuilder` and measures how deep any point lies below that hull's surface (a dent shows as its depth,
  slivers no longer matter). Unit test `body_hull_convexity` (box 0, dent 0.200 m, point on a face 0, flat piece
  rejected).
- **Round-trip mesh comparison failed although nothing changed.** The writer stores one vertex array per primitive,
  so vertex arrays of multi-material parts differ; the test compares triangle corners (position, normal, material)
  in primitive order instead.

- **(steep) The lake fix moved the wall inside the outline.** Terrain there was below the bed clamp, so the max()
  shelf was missing; solution `h = max(min(h, bed), level + 0.3 - 0.5 d)` inside the outline.
- **(steep) 64 m cliff at z = -1000.** The coast cap ended at its 400 m reach while the land polygon edge was only
  120 m outside the bake; solution: cap fades out over 220..400 m and the polygon corners moved to ±2000.
- **(steep) Falls Pool 14 m step, waterfall faces in the test.** Gorge cap `min(h, level + 1 - 2 d)`; `level_slopes`
  skips cells within width + 6 m of waterfall lips (the falls are meant to be vertical).
- **(steep) Switchback road-leg wall of 9 m at (-718, -653).** In-place relaxation did not converge in 24 passes;
  solution: double-buffered Jacobi passes (up to 64, stops when nothing moves) in `bakeCutBanks`.
- **(steep) Perf numbers under other agents' load.** One merged beauty_views run had load ~17 and display-capped
  values (Low lake 62 fps); discarded, re-run twice when the machine was quieter.

## Limitations and open questions

- The real gamepad on the test Mac was only used to find the pad-selection bug; nobody drove with it.
- Grass fade and the tree cross-fade were checked on still captures only, not in motion by eye.
- Rocks and props have one mesh each and no LOD system, so there is no "object detail distance" setting.
- The perf numbers carry background load (a VM, Spotlight); the village view varied by up to 2× between runs.
- Windows / Linux: CI build and headless tests only, never run on real hardware.
- Clump cards show library clusters, not each card's own cluster; they were judged on still captures only. The
  slimmer Poly Haven firs cast less shadow than the old cone trees, so the forest floor reads lighter.
- Waterfalls are judged on still captures; seen from above the sheet still reads as a white panel. Mist cards are
  crossed quads without a scene-depth fade (they fade against the heightfield only). No talus boulders yet.
- Ultra misses 60 fps in the forest (37–48 fps) with the model trees; High reached 60 in 2 of 3 runs there.
  No GPU-side breakdown per pass was measured (the timer is unusable under the 60 fps cap).
- Not done in this stage (planned): water (D), sky (E), post effects (F: TAA, GTAO, DOF, motion blur, auto exposure,
  AgX / LUT, lens effects, contact shadows).
- Attract drive: the gameplay sounds stay muted behind the start menu as before (only the music plays), so the
  drive itself is silent. Start recreates the vehicle, which the audio feed turns into the usual engine-start event;
  not checked by ear.
- Map: the gamepad map controls were checked with a virtual SDL pad only, not with a real pad by hand. The raster is
  1.09 m/px, so at the closest zoom the ground is soft (lines and labels stay sharp). Rocks and tree crowns on the
  raster are stamped discs, not the real shapes. Waypoints are not saved between sessions.

- Traced cars: paint is per 2 cm triangle, so colour borders are stepped, and colours of accessories drawn over the
  body in one view (bull bar over the hood front in the plan, cladding and handles in the side view) land on the
  panels as dark patches the sheet does not have. Nobody has judged the cars by eye beyond the captures listed above.
- The EV rear view stays at IoU 0.8875 (target 0.9): the sheet draws the end-view tires ~0.42 m wide (physics
  0.285 m) and the roof band and rear underbody differently from the side view. The SUV side view is 0.8998.
- The hood scoop / vent of the plan is paint only (the hood surface follows the straight side-view line).
- The tracer depends on hand-placed keypoints; another sheet needs new ones.
- Paint regions: where the sheet's views disagree
  at a corner (SUV hood front corners, EV rear quarter above the wheel) a small patch takes the colour of the view
  that wins there. The paint polygons are hand-drawn from the sheet (pixel accuracy about 1–2 px, 7–13 mm). No
  panel-line pass beyond the existing door shut lines. Nobody has judged the cars by eye beyond the captures.

- **(beauty post)** All looks were judged on still captures only, not in motion by eye (TAA ghosting, motion blur,
  moving clouds and cloud shadows, waves). Nobody has looked at it on Windows / Linux (CI builds only, untested on
  real hardware); the new shader code was compiled to SPIR-V / MSL here and to DXIL only by CI.
- DOF costs ~4 ms at 2880×1800 (menus / garage only by default; `dof_driving` makes it a driving cost).
- SSR misses what is off screen or hidden (falls back to the sky); the refracted bed in the scene copy is already
  fogged, so distant water gets a little less fog than before on the bed part. Waves do not follow the river current.
- The atmosphere is a flat-earth single-scattering approximation without multiple scattering; night and twilight stay
  palette colours. Valley fog is one layer above sea level, not per valley.
- Not done (leftovers): horizon (B), talus boulders at the canyon wall foot, more strata on the canyon walls, darker
  forest floor. No ASan run for this stage (the changes are renderer-side; the headless tests do not touch them).
- (buildings) Frame cost is within the run-to-run noise on Low / Medium (120 fps cap) and the stress pass; on High
  and Ultra the second pair showed +4 / +8 ms GPU, the first +0.8 / −0.5 ms, so the cost there is not pinned down
  (no per-pass breakdown).
- (buildings) No CC0 glTF props were added: the kit covers fences, benches, gardens, poles, tents and woodpiles.
- (buildings) Everything was judged on still captures only. No interiors; windows are dark glass with the model
  shader's sky reflection. Ladder rungs, rails between posts, wires and antennas above 6 m are render-only.
- (buildings) The castle ruins group was moved ~37 m NE as a whole by the placement (its POI teleport point lies in its
  hull); the boathouse floor is 4 m below the Alpine Lake level on a low bank outside the lake polygon.

- Blender body: the test body is a scripted blocky base (proportions of the SUV, no detail); it proves the path, it
  is not a shipped design. The shipped SUV and EV keep their procedural bodies.
- The game draws only LOD0 of a body.glb; LOD1 / LOD2 are written and validated (LOD2 points within 10 mm are
  warnings) but not used yet. Textures in the Blender export are ignored (colour-only contract).
- A glb body is not refitted to tire sizes set outside the definition (scenario `wheels`); validation covers the
  definition, its kits and the ride-height range only.
- Mud shading regions of a glb body are estimated (rest hub height from the static sag g / (2πf)², side |x| at sill
  height); not compared with the procedural values beyond the captures.
- Blender 5.3 on this Mac only (headless `-b --factory-startup`); the scripts were not run from BlenderMCP in this
  stage.

- (steep) The square bake edge still shows from the overview (fog / colour mismatch; no trees on the horizon ring).
  Cliff slabs seen edge-on from above at the Falls Pool rim read as dark shards. Canyon terrace risers stay up to
  ~7 m near-vertical (intended, inside the `level_slopes` limit). Strata are a shader effect on the heightfield, not
  geometry; talus boulders are props on the same heightfield (collision = their model meshes).
- (steep) The perf table is from a shared machine: two runs differ by up to 40 fps in single cells (Medium lake 79
  vs 120); High forest is 66 in both. No GTAO half-res change was made (AO cost measured as small).
- (steep) Everything judged on still captures; nobody drove the new banks with a gamepad.
