## Problems and solutions

- **IRONCLAD arch clearance.** Liner faces over the tire and lips over the outer tire shoulder; plus the default garage
  lowering. Raise-only ride height, lip flare, liner faces deleted (see 00:15). No converter bypass.
- **Coil-over through the bonnet.** Layout made for the SUV frame. Hub-relative layout + skin test (00:30).
- **Background builds killed.** Detached `cmake --build` jobs died when the shell call returned; builds run in the
  foreground.
- **Off-road identity of the tire model regained where it had drifted:** `minitrack` now prints exactly the pre-tire-model
  output (12 lines differed at f40c766); the deflection heat uses ground speed, not wheel speed, so a spinning wheel is
  heated by its slip power only.
- (ironwall) **Misclassified infield islands.** Cause: the converter read the source material name
  (`Centro_Pasto`, Spanish for "centre grass") instead of its texture (worn asphalt). Solution: map it to asphalt.
  Six untouched KTX2 maps came out byte-different on the regeneration (encoder nondeterminism); they were restored
  from git to avoid LFS churn.
- **Blank discs rendered white chrome.** The IRONCLAD bake turns untextured slots white and the grille part is
  chrome. The blank material got a 4 x 4 texture in the blank colour (both bakes read it).
- **Number plates vanished.** `ManufacturerPlate` is the plate itself; repainted to its background instead of
  deleted.
- **Spare-cover star missed.** It sits in the palette mesh next to a dark panel; zone tightened to the star (the
  panel is not wholly inside, so it stays).
- (mapedge) **NW white band.** Cause: snow albedo averaged into the ring's edge tone and faded over ~3 km, plus a
  fixed 720 m ring snow line far above the level's 182 m. Solution: no snow in the edge tone; the ring's snow comes
  from the level's snow line per pixel.
- (mapedge) **Square edge from above.** Cause: the ring was the bake square scaled outward with straight coast lines out
  of the corners. Solution: ring rounded into a circle, noisy coast continuing the bake's sea / land, 24 km out.
- (mapedge) **Open sea rendered as a pale sand film (own intermediate bug, never pushed).** Cause: the minimum-land lift
  `max(h, 8 * smoothstep(0.5, 1, land))` is `max(h, 0)` on sea columns, so the seabed sat at y = 0 under the water
  plane (zero depth through the refraction path). Solution: only land is lifted, easing in from the coastline.
- (mapedge) **Sea at the edge not found by `waterKindAt`.** The bake tags `WaterKind::Sea` only within 80 m of its
  coast; open sea at the edge is now told by height below 0 m on levels that have any sea water
  (`Level::waterKinds()` accessor).
- (landing audio) **Felt acceleration from position differences was noise** (2.4 g on flat ground). Cause: second
  difference of the body position per 1/120 s tick. Solution: from the solver's center-of-mass velocity
  (`VehicleTelemetry::velocity`), 15 ms low-pass.
- (landing audio) **Scenario spawns at z = -48 rolled** (10-12 km/h after 2.5 s, edge berm); moved to z = -44. The
  SUV cannot climb the 0.45 m step from the steps spawn: that section ends past the 0.30 m step.
- (bump) **Jolt's anti-roll bar rate depends on damping and step.** Cause: its impulse is a velocity bias of the
  suspension constraint. Solution: bars as suspension forces in the per-step rate.
- (bump) **`tire_marks_surfaces` dirt slip 0.29 < 0.3 after the damper change** (0.34 before). The 1.5 s power turn
  was marginal; lengthened to 1.7 s (dirt slip 0.65, all marks on the lane).
- (bump) **`mini_track_lap` expects the mini track as the last feature.** The bump strip is inserted before it
  (17 -> 18 features).
- (bump) **Scenario `teleport bump strip`** matched "Bump sequence" (first name containing the first word); the
  scenario teleports by index 16.
- (bump) **KESTREL beached on the one-sided mound** (low nose stops on its face, no rollover); the scenario runs it
  over the full-width mound only.

## Limitations and open questions

- Licence of both bodies unknown (a commercial game asset names); private repo only, must go before anything is public.
- The bodies show the real cars' designs; the badges render as small unreadable shapes at vertex-colour resolution,
  but the grille star / roundel shapes are recognisable. Not painted out.
  (01:50: painted out, see Stages 01:05-01:35.) The steering-wheel roundel disc (KESTREL) is only checked in the
  prep log: no camera sees the steering wheels through the tinted glass. The grille and body designs themselves
  remain recognisable.
- KESTREL 0-100 4.96 s vs the published 4.1 s (no launch control in the probe; rear-tire limited).
- Nobody has judged either car by ear or by hand on a pad; the coil-over layout under a Blender body is a generic
  rule, not the real cars' geometry. Prep scripts live outside the repo (owner's machine).
- Tires: no target was forced. Slicks reach 80 C within 1-2 laps in no lap test, not even on the KESTREL with the limit
  driver (61 C after 3 laps); they reach it only at a sustained limit (circle, 150 s) or off warmers. The fitted
  coefficients are for F1 / FSAE tires; no wheel-arch shielding of convection; the autopilot does not trail-brake or
  rotate the car like a human at the limit. Probably still 15-25 K below track-day readings; not verified against
  measured data for these cars.
- ROAD / SLICK construction mass is x0.6 / x0.5 of the vehicle's own tire; for a road car (KESTREL, own tire already a
  street tire) this makes its road set lighter than the real tire.
- (ironwall) The surface choice per material is still by hand; only the drift area is covered by the test (the
  paddock and the verges are not swept).
- (mapedge) The horizon ring is render-only (no collision); the play limit and the perimeter wall inside the bake are
  unchanged. Distant islands read as flat plateaus with a beach rim from far above. The ring is still low detail
  (16-sample columns, rings 15 % apart): coarse up close if a free camera flies past the edge. The ~0.6 ms cost
  from very high cameras was not optimised further (merging one noise fetch did not measurably help).
- (landing audio) Nobody has listened to the new body rattle / knock: all claims are from capture analysis and the
  headless test. The rattle and knock are synthesized (no recordings of real panel rattle); the levels were set by
  measurement (hard landing up to ~+5 dB over the bed for 250 ms, typical event well below it), not by ear. No top-out
  sound: Jolt's wheels have no unsprung mass, so full rebound is not an event in the simulation. Only the SUV and the
  buggy were driven in the scenario; EV, IRONCLAD and KESTREL use the `suv` / `coupe` presets untested by ear.
- (bump) The IRONCLAD (8-10 cm bump travel, body hits) still flips over a 1 m kicker or a step with the throttle held
  or floored; the patrol (750 kg) with the throttle floored. Variations of knee, tire rate, hysteresis, damping and
  preload changed nothing robustly; these are geometric, accepted and documented. A 1 m 0.35 m kicker has a 47 deg
  face and acts as a ramp.
- (bump) Not modelled: drivetrain / brake reaction torque on the body, tire relaxation length and load sensitivity,
  tire deflection in series with the spring before the suspension is solid. The bump-stop hysteresis 0.5 is an
  assumption. Saved garage setups with old anti-roll values (Jolt label units, e.g. 400) now mean 400 N/m at the
  wheel, i.e. almost no bar. Nobody has driven the new suspension by hand.
- (setups) A dropped anti-roll value loses the user's intent (a very stiff bar); the user has to set it again on
  the slider, now in N/m.
