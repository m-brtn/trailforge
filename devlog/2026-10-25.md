  ~0.5 for ~1.5 s; the tests drove the builtin config, not the shipped SUV, so they missed it); (2) the feed
  envelope (release 0.15 s + gate-off hysteresis) and the mixer release added ~0.35-0.45 s; (3) the onset one-shot
  always played its 1.69 s file to the end, which was most of the tail after a brake tap. Solution: a separate,
  stricter `squealSlip` gate, faster envelopes, the one-shot tracked per wheel (voice serial) and faded with it.
- **(skid-audio) The builtin vehicle's power slide went silent with a longitudinal-only ratio gate.** Cause: its
  front wheels plow sideways at 6.6 m/s with a combined ratio of 0.53. Solution: combine the longitudinal and
  lateral ratios on an ellipse (lateral onset 0.40); power slide 0.18-0.19.
- **(skid-audio) A windowed check read a stuck throttle (0.59).** Cause: a packaged TrailForge.app running at the
  same time with a gamepad. Solution: rerun after no trailforge process was left.
- **(skid-audio, part 2) audio_check timed out at the puddle splash (line 48).** Cause: since v0.9.0 the SUV
  starts in 2H and bogs down on the mud path (0.9 km/h at full throttle at x -9, z 63), before and after the
  keyboard ramp. Solution: `transfer 4h` for the mud section, `transfer 2h` after it; splash at z 72.8 m, 44 km/h.
- **(skid-audio, part 2) First SUV profile was louder and not darker enough above 3 kHz.** Cause: scrub noise band
  up to 1.4 kHz at level 0.9 was +4.8 dB over the squeal and leaked to 3 kHz (+3.2 % energy above 3 kHz). Solution:
  scrub band 120-900 Hz, level 0.6, squeal low-pass max 3.2 kHz: level -0.6 dB vs sporty, >3 kHz 0.8 %.
- **(skid-audio, part 3) No way to script a full-pedal launch.** Cause: W / S ramp since KeyboardPedals. Solution:
  scenario command `pedal throttle|brake V`, committed first so the "before" build for the A/B has it.
- **(skid-audio, part 3) Brake 1.0 + full throttle is no burnout.** Cause: the 3000 N*m rear brakes and the open
  differential alternate a ~3.5-4 m/s spin between the rear wheels (no marks before or after). Solution: the probe,
  test and captures use brake 0.6, where both rears spin at ~10 m/s and the car moves 0.1 m in 5 s.
- **(skid-audio, part 3) First tuning left the 2H full-pedal launch unmarked.** Cause: its tread heat peaks at 0.14
  and the first laydown curve (smoothstep 0.08..0.75) mapped that to ~0.02. Solution: linear laydown, full at H 0.5;
  width full at H 0.45 so the 5 s burnout spot is full width.

- **(v0.11.0) EV front bumper center missing, white body showing through.** Cause: `wrapBumper` walked the corner
  arcs in the wrong order, so the straight center segment crossed the vehicle 14 cm inside the body; the SUV rear
  bumper had the same bug (only its corners were visible). Solution: angle order fixed; model test: outward
  triangles spanning x = 0 at y 0.12 must reach |z| > 2.28 for every catalog model.
- **(v0.11.0) Hood accent on one fender top only.** Cause: `k >= 12` never matched the last side segment. Solution:
  `k >= 11` (also changes the SUV).
- **(v0.11.0) Scenario `vehicle ID` then `expect electric == 1` failed.** Cause: the new config applies on the next
  tick. Solution: `wait 0.2` after `vehicle`.
- **(v0.11.0) ev_check lift-off regen and reverse checks failed.** Causes: after a `capture` the sim lags the script
  clock, so a timed step started early; starting from the menu applies settings and re-enabled `auto_reverse`.
  Solution: `wait 0.3` after captures before timed steps; `set auto_reverse 0` after the drive-off.
- **(v0.11.0) `menus.txt`: Start skipped the vehicle page after `menu intro`.** Cause: `menu intro` rebuilt
  `GameFlow` without the vehicle list. Solution: keep the ids across the reset.
- **(v0.11.0) Battery energy in float loses small steps.** Cause: 1/120 s of 0.8 kW is ~7 J against a 4.3e8 J
  pack, below float resolution (~32 J there). Solution: battery energy in double.
- **(v0.12.0 prelude 1) `ev_check` motor power at 0.5 s and two headless EV tests failed with TC on.** Cause: they
  measured the raw drivetrain under free front-wheel spin; TC trims the first ~0.5 s (motor power down to 17 kW).
  Retuning the EV TC rates (cut 4-8 /s, recover 1-6 /s) left that window unchanged and the 3 s speed within 1.5 km/h,
  so the tuning stays. Solution: `ev_simulation` and `audio_electric` pin TC off for the raw-drivetrain checks (and
  `ev_simulation` adds a TC-on launch check); `ev_check` checks the motors at 2 s.
- **(v0.12.0 prelude 1) One `full_check` run failed 6 low-range checks (L accepted while "moving").** Not
  reproduced: the rerun of the same binary passed 245 / 0. Looks like a script-clock timing flake (the SUV TC default
  did not change); noted, not fixed.

- **(v0.12.0) Roads dipped 0.2-0.45 m below their profile at isolated points.** Cause: roads are carved in 60 m
  pieces blended by weight^16; just past a piece's inner end its clamped end segment still had weight ~1 (the cut /
  fill shoulder is wide) and pulled the surface toward the end height. Solution: pieces overlap by 10 samples, and a
  point whose nearest point on a piece is that piece's inner end ignores the piece. Worst center deviation 0.45 m ->
  0.033 m, cross-section 0.44 m -> 0.057 m; test thresholds tightened to 0.08 / 0.12 m.
- **(v0.12.0) `level_vehicles` crashed in the vehicle loader.** Cause: adding `SurfaceId::Snow` grew
  `kSurfaceCount`, but the `surfaceGrip` key table had 7 names (null pointer) and the builtin `tireSurfaceGrip`
  default had 7 values (snow grip 0). Solution: key `snow` in the table, in all three vehicle files, the README format
  table and the default array.
- **(v0.12.0) Parked vehicles rolled on the level.** Cause: the test parked without brakes on road grades up to 22 %.
  Solution: brakes held while parking. The lake teleport snaps onto the Lake Trail facing along the road, so a
  straight drive never reached the water; the test now places the vehicle on a searched shore point facing 32 m of
  lake deeper than 1.5 m (`Simulation::requestPlace`).

## Limitations and open questions
- (v0.3.0) The sand area has a straight edge to the meadow (per-triangle material, no blend). Sand is rigid.
- (v0.3.0) The soil print length was measured on synthetic samples; in the app it was only looked at in captures.
- (spray) Judged only from still captures, never watched in motion by a person. Particles do not collide with the
  vehicle, rocks or each other and take no material from the ground. In the puddle capture the water drops are hard to
  tell apart from the mud clods. Spray is updated per frame (frame time capped at 50 ms), not per physics tick, and is
  not part of the replayable state.

- (friction) The μ values are plausible ranges, not measured tire data. Surfaces stay rigid: mud and grass are only
  lower friction; there is no sinkage, no rolling resistance, no bulldozing, and spinning tires do not dig in.
- (friction) Hill starts with full throttle fail on 15° / 20° mud because the spinning tire is on the sliding part of
  the curve (μ × 0.80); a careful throttle was not tried. No traction control.
- (friction) The 35° hill on packed dirt (μ 0.70 ≈ tan 35°) is still climbed in `full_check` (6.6–13.2 km/h on the
  face at z = 21.4 in five runs, `hill35` log). The grip margin there is about zero, so the climb depends on the run-up; this
  was not measured separately.
- (friction) At 60 Hz a full-throttle concrete launch still slips for 1–2 steps on the front wheels (see Problems).
- (friction) Handling was not driven by hand after the retune; cornering grip on concrete dropped from 1.15 to 0.95.

- (package) Ad-hoc signed, not notarized: a downloaded copy needs right-click → Open or removing the quarantine
  attribute. The quarantine / Gatekeeper path itself was not exercised (the zip was not downloaded through a
  browser). Built for macOS 27.0 only (minos 27.0); no older macOS was tried.

- (reverse) With auto reverse a held S no longer holds the vehicle on a slope (it backs down after 0.25 s);
  hill holding needs the handbrake or auto reverse off. The brake-hold checks of `full_check`, and `tracks`,
  `audio_check`, `suspension` run with `set auto_reverse 0`, i.e. the classic mapping.
- (reverse) Scenario stops "hold S until speed_kmh < 0.5" can outlast the 0.25 s hold while the body still rocks,
  which is why those sections use the classic mapping. Gamepad triggers were not tested (no gamepad connected);
  they feed the same pedals as W/S. Not driven by hand.

- The wider track (1.80 m) changes handling slightly (more roll stiffness from geometry); only the existing
  scenario checks and CTest were rerun, no new handling measurements.
- (aids) ABS and TC are simplified control logic: they read the true contact-point ground speed (no estimation),
  run every physics tick (coarser at 60 Hz: on dirt the straight ABS stop is ~7 % longer than with locked wheels),
  and act only through brake torque and a throttle factor. Both are off by default.
- (aids) "Locked" is a Jolt LSD with ratio 1.05, a torque bias, not a rigid link: open + rear + center locks does
  not leave the pit slope (2.4 m), only all three locks do. TC On fails the 20° grass hill start that TC off and
  Off-road climb; on the flat, TC reduces wheelspin but also v(2 s) on grass and mud.
- (aids) Combined slip is on by default and changes the locked-wheel behavior (no steering); the existing
  scenarios and skid checks pass, but handling was not driven by hand. Gamepad D-pad bindings not tested (no pad).
- (aids) Steered ABS stops on grass / mud cross into the neighboring lanes (8–12 m sideways), so those headings
  include other surfaces.
- (aids) The `tap C` flake is not explained (see Problems).
- (aids review) TC at 60 Hz is worse than TC off on the 15° mud hill (slides back 3–7 m with On); use 120 Hz or
  more. Hill-start results near the grip limit are chaotic (one run can flip with float rounding), hence sweeps.
- (aids review) The friction ellipse fades in between 1 and 3 m/s of contact-patch speed, so it does nothing at a
  crawl; it is a simple normalized-slip ellipse, not a tire model (no load sensitivity, no relaxation length).
- (aids review) The 0.45 m step climb from a stop with the car wedged between the steps (rear on the 0.30 m step,
  fronts at the face) can stall; `full_check` now starts that climb on the 0.30 m step.
- (aids review) `analyze_capture.py` was not run on this review's capture (numpy is not installed here).

- (spray-look) Judged from still captures only; nobody watched the spray or the body mud in motion. Body mud regions are
  fixed shapes around the arches in body space, not deposits of particles hitting the body; the particles still do
  not collide with the vehicle. The grass blade specks on the tire are hard to see in shadowed captures.
- (spray-look) Perf numbers come from a noisy session (60 Hz display, other agents running builds); Low / Medium
  follow and overview were 0.3-0.9 ms higher than the base run while High / Ultra were equal or lower. CPU avg was
  higher in the new runs (0.7-1.0 vs 0.1-0.2 ms in the base run of the same session; the v0.3.0 runs earlier showed
  0.5-1.2 ms for the base); an alternating A / B later showed no difference (see Results).

- (grass v0.5.0) Judged from still captures only; nobody watched the wind or the flowers in motion or judged the look
  by hand-driving. Gust motion is inferred from three frames 0.4 s apart.
- (grass v0.5.0) Medium and Low still look like scattered blades from the high overview camera (as before: the
  blade count per preset did not change); the ground canopy colour hides the gaps but not the sprinkled look.
- (grass v0.5.0) Ultra costs up to +1.9 ms GPU in the meadow / wheat view (alpha-cut shapes disable early depth
  rejection for the grass draw, and the strips have 8 instead of 7 triangles at LOD 0). Not optimized further.
- (grass v0.5.0) Wheat ears and awns turn their wide side to the camera (a billboard around the stalk axis);
  stalks are not tested against each other or against the vehicle body, only the wheel contacts and the trample
  mask flatten them. Flattened stalks lie in a random / outward direction, not along the driving direction.
- (grass v0.5.0) The meadow near the north wall is in the wall's shadow at sunset (sun elevation 2-8°), so the
  translucency glow shows only in sunlit parts.
- (v0.6.0) Windows and Linux were never run on real hardware; the D3D12 / DXIL path has not been run at all (no
  Windows GPU in CI). Linux was only run on lavapipe (software, ~6 FPS) and MoltenVK on the Mac.
- (v0.6.0) glslang's HLSL front end is deprecated upstream (issue #4210; CMake prints a warning). If it is removed,
  switch SPIR-V generation to DXC on Windows / Linux hosts; macOS has no DXC build.
- (v0.6.0) Vulkan: shadow depth bias is unclamped (SDL does not enable `depthBiasClamp`); not compared visually
  against Metal beyond the MoltenVK captures. GPU timing is n/a on Vulkan / D3D12.
- (v0.6.0) Three pre-existing gcc `-Wformat-truncation` warnings (DebugUI gear name, AudioSystem status); harmless.
- (v0.7.0) Gamepad menu navigation was not tested (no gamepad connected); only keyboard and mouse went through the
  real SDL event path in scenarios. Nobody listened to the music or the UI sounds by ear; the levels come from the
  mixer read-outs. `analyze_capture.py` was not run (numpy not installed).
- (v0.7.0) Interpretation of the pause audio: the gameplay sounds stop with the simulation; the day / night
  ambience beds keep playing at full level (not simulation-driven; the previous half duck was removed because the
  brief says no ducking); no music in gameplay or pause. `assets/audio/ambience/menu_*` stays unused.
- (v0.7.0) The world ticks behind the intro and the start menu (vehicle idles at the spawn); Start resets the
  vehicle, so the engine-start sound plays when gameplay begins. The settings file is applied on start and when
  a Settings page closes; F1-F3 panel changes are session-only and are overwritten by the next Settings change of
  the same value. Scenarios never read or write the settings file.
- (v0.7.0) The menus are only checked at the 2880x1800 window of this Mac; small windows and ultra-wide aspect
  ratios were not looked at.

- (v0.8.0) Garage gamepad navigation untested (no gamepad); keyboard and mouse went through the real SDL event path.
  Garage layout only checked at 2880x1800.
- (v0.8.0) "Locked" centre (4H / 4L, AWD lock) is a limited-slip ratio of 1.05, not a rigid shaft: no driveline
  wind-up in tight turns on grip. On-demand is a centre LSD with a small front share, not a clutch with a
  controller; for the SUV (front share 0.5) on-demand behaves like AWD.
- (v0.8.0) Compression / rebound damping uses the previous tick's suspension velocity sign (one-step lag); bump
  stops are a stiffness ramp; Ackermann only lowers the outer wheel's maximum angle; drag acts at the COM (no lift,
  no pitch moment).
- (v0.8.0) Kits are supported by the format but the SUV ships with only factory parts; a second real vehicle is not
  included (rwd_test is test-only). Electric motor drivetrains are rejected by validation.
- (v0.9.0) Modal and wheel scrolling: gamepad path untested (no gamepad); trackpad only through synthetic SDL wheel
  events (fractional steps are added the same way). The rename field has no caret movement (Left / Right only drop
  the selection) and no N/mm input for the springs.
- **(v0.9.0) Tire size vs the concept (proposal, not applied):** the sheet scaled by the wheelbase suggests a
  ~0.90 m tire (radius ~0.45 m) against the physics 0.80 m (radius 0.40); wheelbase / diameter 3.1 on the sheet vs
  3.5. A later tire kit could set `wheel.radius` 0.45 (the body already fits it: `model_wheel_clearance` passes with
  0.45 / 0.30). Width: the sheet is not readable enough for a number; 0.30 m kept.
- **(v0.9.0)** No antennas on any car (deliberate deviation from the concept sheet, user request).
- **(v0.9.0)** Remaining mismatches (README "Vehicle rendering"): ground to roof 2.0 m vs ~1.75 m on the sheet,
