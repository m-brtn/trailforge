## Problems and solutions

- **"Slippery everywhere".** Cause: not the friction values (no hidden halving either: the vehicle's combine
  callback replaces Jolt's geometric mean, μ = tire curve x surface x tire set x temperature) but (1) a held steering
  key steered 35 deg at any speed: at 60 km/h the SUV needs about 9 deg at the asphalt limit and 5.5 deg on grass, so
  the fronts slid and the car pushed wide at 72-76 % of its grip; (2) most of Exploration Peaks is low-μ ground by
  design (grass 54 %, sand 21 %, snow 10 % of the bake; roads gravel). Solution: the speed-dependent keyboard range.
  Surfaces, tire curves and tire temperature were not changed.

- **(body audio) Problem: the squeal in real slides had to stay identical while the mixer changed.** Cause: the
  squeal's per-slide pitch and loop start come from the mixer's shared random sequence, which the rattle grains also
  consumed. Solution: body synthesis has its own generator; squeal code paths untouched.
- **(body audio) Problem: another agent edited the same audio files concurrently.** Solution: the user stopped it;
  squeal-changing parts reverted, the rest reviewed and reworked. Other agents' uncommitted work in the tree (water,
  menu, level, shader) is not part of the body audio commits.

## Limitations and open questions

- (body audio) Nobody has listened to the new body sounds; all statements above come from tests and capture analysis.
- (body audio) The audio_check sample peak (-3.2 dBFS) comes from a metal impact recording plus a thud at the same
  moment; not compared with v0.29.0 (truncated baseline).
- (body audio) The thud puts 24-35 % of a jolt's energy below 200 Hz (v0.29.0 < 10 %): intended as a short low hit,
  but an earlier report (2026-10-27) disliked a long, loud low thud; needs a listening check.
- (body audio) The capture analyzer's "decay to -20 dB" per event is dominated by the engine bed in driving captures
  (565-600 ms before and after); the headless renders are the meaningful decay numbers.

  window text in `src/ui/DebugUI.cpp` ("Tire sets and temperature", now `docs/vehicles.md`).
