## Problems and solutions

- **"Slippery everywhere".** Cause: not the friction values (no hidden halving either: the vehicle's combine
  callback replaces Jolt's geometric mean, μ = tire curve x surface x tire set x temperature) but (1) a held steering
  key steered 35 deg at any speed: at 60 km/h the SUV needs about 9 deg at the asphalt limit and 5.5 deg on grass, so
  the fronts slid and the car pushed wide at 72-76 % of its grip; (2) most of Exploration Peaks is low-μ ground by
  design (grass 54 %, sand 21 %, snow 10 % of the bake; roads gravel). Solution: the speed-dependent keyboard range.
  Surfaces, tire curves and tire temperature were not changed.
- **Held key overshot the peak on loose ground.** Cause: the 01:27 range used the tire's asphalt peak on every
  surface, so on grass / gravel / dirt the held key steered for a 0.9-1.0 g corner. Solution: the range follows the
  smoothed grip under the front wheels (01:50). Surfaces, tires and vehicles not changed.

- **(body audio) Problem: the squeal in real slides had to stay identical while the mixer changed.** Cause: the
  squeal's per-slide pitch and loop start come from the mixer's shared random sequence, which the rattle grains also
  consumed. Solution: body synthesis has its own generator; squeal code paths untouched.
- **(body audio) Problem: another agent edited the same audio files concurrently.** Solution: the user stopped it;
  squeal-changing parts reverted, the rest reviewed and reworked. Other agents' uncommitted work in the tree (water,
  menu, level, shader) is not part of the body audio commits.
- **(water) Sawtooth shoreline and slabs.** Cause: (1) the river surface rule "below the lowest terrain across the
  channel, never rising downstream" leaves flat runs and then drops of up to ~1.3 m (terraces, e.g. Glacier Creek
  122.87 m for 20 m, then 121.52); (2) on steep reaches the cones of the downstream segments carve the banks below
  the local water level (Glacier Creek below the ford: 2.4 m deep, ground at the edge of the reach ~2 m under the
  surface), so 1486 wet river samples had a dry 4-neighbour more than 5 cm below their surface; (3) the water mesh put
  dry corners at the mean of the wet ones, so the water ended in mid-air along cell edges, and cells whose corners
  differed by more than 1 m were skipped as "waterfalls". Solution: `bakeRivers` takes a running mean of the surface
  over +-10 m (pins and drops >= 2 m bound the windows, pins exact, at most 0.3 m above the old rule); `bakeWater`
  limits river water to 0.5 m per m from the edge of its reach; the mesh puts a dry corner 5 cm under its own ground
  when that is below the wet mean, and skips steep cells only within 3 m of a waterfall. Walls 1486 -> 728 (the rest
  are within a cell of the reach edge and now meet the ground inside the cell).
- (scenarios) Canyon Road bridge failure. Cause: since ee67b63 (suspension force rework: SUV damping 0.45/0.45 ->
  0.35/0.55, bump stops, tire compliance) the SUV turns while launching from standstill at 0.45 throttle on the 2 deg
  gravel cross-slope 20 m before the deck: heading 93.35 deg (as placed, the deck axis) -> 89.6 deg within 16 m
  (0-30 km/h), then straight on the flat deck. With no steering it reached the deck 1 m off the axis, 3.7 deg off
  its line, met the left railing past x 710, was thrown up at x 723 (RR 49 kN, tilt 20 deg, four wheels in the air)
  and stayed wedged at x 733.9 (`max_air` 4, `road_dist` 45). Before ee67b63 the same launch kept the placed heading.
  On flat concrete (proving ground skid strip) the SUV launches straight (heading 180.0 deg for 4 s), so it is not
  a left / right asymmetry and the deck and level bake are not involved. Solution: the block starts at the foot of the
  ramp on the deck axis (`place 685 -125.2 86.6`, was 668 -126.2), still crossing the ramp-to-deck joint; checks
  unchanged (`max_air == 0`, `speed_kmh > 25`, `d_y > -0.5`, `road_dist < 3`). The Coast Road bridge block keeps
  its 20 m gravel approach and passes.

- (iOS) Black screen, `SDL_main` never called. Cause: SDK 27 apps need the UIScene life cycle; SDL 3.4.18 has a scene
  delegate but iOS only connects it with `UIApplicationSceneManifest` in Info.plist. Solution: the manifest
  (`SDLSceneConfiguration`, `SDLUIKitSceneDelegate`).
- (iOS Simulator) `Device does not meet the hardware requirements for SDL_GPU Metal`, then a GPU stall in the first
  frames. Cause: the Simulator's Metal device reports only Apple2 (no Apple3 / Apple7 / Mac2 / Common3, no BC) and has
  no depth clip mode, which SDL sets on every pipeline bind. Solution: SDL patch inside `#if TARGET_OS_SIMULATOR`
  (accept the device, log its families, skip `setDepthClipMode`); depth is clipped there, no clamp.
- (iOS) First ImGui draw: "renderPipelineState must be set". Cause: imgui_impl_sdlgpu3_shaders.h tests
  `TARGET_OS_MAC` (1 on every Apple OS) before `TARGET_OS_IPHONE`, so iOS gets the macOS metallibs. Solution: ImGui
  patch, MSL source on iOS (also needed on a real device).
- (iOS Simulator) Crash on return from the background ("Connection to SimMetalHost XPC service was lost"). Cause: the
  Simulator's GPU host process SimMetalHost segfaulted (newBufferWithLength / submitCommandBuffer / deleteObject); it
  also crashed when the Simulator window opened and twice while Settings and Photos launched with TrailForge not
  running, so it is not caused by the game. Possibly the CoreSimulator / Xcode version mismatch above (not verified).
  Not solved; `run_simulator.sh` keeps the device headless unless `--gui`. One earlier run hung on its first frame
  right after the window opened (SimMetalHost crash at the same second).

- (packaging) The 0.29.0 app (local and the published v0.29.0 zip) is refused by LaunchServices on macOS 27.0.1:
  Info.plist `LSMinimumSystemVersion` 27.2 while the binary has minos 27.0. Cause: no `CMAKE_OSX_DEPLOYMENT_TARGET`,
  so `cmake/AppIcon.cmake` took `xcrun --sdk macosx --show-sdk-version` (27.2, a newer SDK than the 27.0 SDK the
  compiler linked against). Solution: macOS deployment target 14.0 by default in `CMakeLists.txt` (iOS presets keep
  26.0; MSL 3.0 needs 13+, metallib target was already 14.0 and now follows the variable), plist = deployment target
  (configure error if empty), `package_macos.sh` fails if `LSMinimumSystemVersion` is above the binary minos or the
  host macOS.

- (handling) **SUV somersault over hills at 80 km/h.** Cause: since ee67b63 the tire stage (suspension solid, the
  wheel pushing into the tire) was damped at 5 % of critical, and Jolt's wheel is massless, so a bottoming returned
  ~85 % of its energy to the body: a 1.5 m drop rebounded 15 %, a crest compression threw the body up (takeoff 6.1
  m/s on a 1 m x 12 m hill at 80 km/h) nose-up and it landed on the roof. Solution: `wheel.vertical.damping`, default
  1.0 of critical (stands for the unsprung-mass impact, carcass damping and bump stop hysteresis that are not
  modelled; chosen, not measured), and the damper's high-speed slope 0.75 instead of 0.5 (measured below).

- (scenarios) **Is the SUV's 3.7 deg turn launching before the Canyon Road bridge honest?** Measured: the spot is not
  a 2 deg cross-slope (earlier note) but a gravel road bench, grade 2.6 %, camber 1.1 % at x 664 twisting to 0.4 %
  at x 672 (residuals to the best plane +-20 mm), berm and ditch only beyond +-3 m of the axis. The turn is still 3.7
  deg after today's changes (app: heading 93.35 -> 89.63 at x 684). It turns left driving east and west over the

  compliance steer (0), damping (0.45 / 0.45), TC mode, locks, a rollback before the launch and the friction ellipse.
  Headless, a copy of the level heights as a lone HeightFieldShape and as a MeshShape both give +2.82 / +2.81 deg (no
  heightfield or tile artifact); ideal planes with the same grades -0.1 deg; 5 mm random noise on that plane -0.14 to
  +0.61 deg (2H); a synthetic camber twist 1.1 -> 0.4 % over 8 m alone +2.0 deg in 2H, -0.2 in 4H, sign reversed
  with the twist, 0 with constant camber. Yaw rate on the twist: throttle 0.45 rises to 1.3 deg/s and decays after
  the 1-2 upshift; 0.7 grows to 11 deg/s in 3 s; 0.2 stays at 0.4 deg/s. Decision: physical behaviour, not an

  amplifies it while the steering is held rigidly at 0; 4H spreads the drive and holds. Other level spots turn
  -4.9 to +4.3 deg in 3 s with either sign in 2H and 4H (wider relief). Physics unchanged; the scenario keeps its
  start on the ramp, its comment corrected. Why ee67b63 changed it is not resolved (see Limitations).

- **(roads) Podium roads.** Cause: the old profile was the midpoint of the largest grade-limited function below the

## Limitations and open questions

- (visual 1) The light split is per pixel luminance (not per colour), quantized to 1/15 for the sun share; AO only
  runs without MSAA (as before). The multi-bounce tint guesses the albedo from the pixel hue. Contact shadows are
  screen space: off-screen occluders cast none, thin objects count as 0.25 m+ thick.
- (visual 2) Only the terrain casts far shadows (trees, rocks, buildings beyond the cascades cast none); texels are
  ~1.4 m and the light bias is several metres, so thin ridges cast slightly short shadows. The horizon ring outside
  the terrain square neither casts nor receives. When the light turns, the far shadow updates in steps of ~0.26 deg.
- (visual 3) The probe has no grass blades, water surfaces, spray or tire marks; its mips are a box filter (not a
  GGX prefilter) and the diffuse is the mean of the coarsest mips, not SH. Faces rendered in different frames come
  from slightly different centres at speed. With MSAA the level foliage in the probe is not alpha-cut.
- (visual 4) Clouds are still flat layers: no parallax or volume, the camera cannot fly into them, the optional
  1/4-resolution raymarched volume was not built (left out to keep the budget for parallax). The sky's cumulus
  detail is not in the ground shadow (the edges of the shadow are softer than the cloud). The cirrus casts no shadow.
- (visual 5) The parallax is a texture shift only: no change to the geometry, collision or silhouette, no self
  shadow, and the lighting normal is the layer normal at the shifted lookup. Tire marks sit on the undisplaced
  surface and make no ruts. Only on the textured level ground (not the proving ground, not with the textures off).
  The five items' costs were measured in separate views, not summed in one frame.
- (water) The steep Glacier Creek reach is a smooth sheet on a ~0.7 slope: no rapids, white water or flow
  direction. The shoreline is piecewise linear per 1 m cell (no sub-cell ground data in the water shader beyond the
  smoothed height map). Lake water outside its polygon (3 walls) unchanged. Exploration Peaks bridge failure at
  x 733.9 (pre-existing in this tree) not investigated. Only looked at by the agent.

- (menus) Only checked on this Mac (Retina, points); on displays without framebuffer scaling the floors follow
  ImGui's content scale, untested. The full-screen map (`MapUI`, `map_check.txt`) and the F1-F3 diagnostics windows
