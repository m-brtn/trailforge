## Problems and solutions

- **"Slippery everywhere".** Cause: not the friction values (no hidden halving either: the vehicle's combine
  callback replaces Jolt's geometric mean, μ = tire curve x surface x tire set x temperature) but (1) a held steering
  key steered 35 deg at any speed: at 60 km/h the SUV needs about 9 deg at the asphalt limit and 5.5 deg on grass, so
  the fronts slid and the car pushed wide at 72-76 % of its grip; (2) most of Exploration Peaks is low-μ ground by
  design (grass 54 %, sand 21 %, snow 10 % of the bake; roads gravel). Solution: the speed-dependent keyboard range.
  Surfaces, tire curves and tire temperature were not changed.
